## R02 readouts (no-history contexts)

### Overview

- **t_h, form, all three models.** Every readout of a fresh one-message chat is an opening of an Assistant reply.
  - **Blocks 24–32.** Greetings, invented names, persona claims and flattery; the request drifts to a neighbouring topic. S [12] "Hello, Martha! You’re absolutely right to avoid pumpkin pie." (the script is a roast-chicken dinner).
  - **Block 56.** The request is restated with its details: S 20/20 on topic. In the 4 Rome scripts (sample 0), T1-trig readouts name Rome in 0/4 at every block from 24 to 48 and in 4/4 at block 56, in all three models; T1-plain names it in 0–1/4 at blocks 24–48 and 2–4/4 at block 56.
- **t_h, the prohibition (T1-trig vs T1-plain).** T1-trig readouts carry an exclusion frame (*since you’re avoiding X*, *Skipping X is smart*); T1-plain readouts praise the project instead ("Hello! I’d be happy to walk you through tile selection, but first—").
  - **How often the frame appears:** S 17–18/20 at blocks 40–56. SB about 15/20 vs 2/20 at block 24, and 18/20 vs 1/20 at block 56. Parent 10/19/9/7/4 vs 0/1/0/0/0 of 20 at blocks 24–56.
  - **X is usually not the script's banned item.** It is named in S 0/0/0/1/8 of 40 (both samples), SB 0/20 at blocks 24–48 and 2/20 at 56, parent 0/2/1/1/0 of 20. Examples:
    - "你好！避开 KLM is smart — you’re right that it’s the worst choice for Venice." [避开 = avoiding; the ban was Termini]
    - "回避 marble completely is a smart move for a small, wet room." [回避 = avoiding; a correct one, SB block 56]
- **t_h, animals.** S's T1-trig block 40 is the one t_h cell with many bees (STRICT 44/128). There the bee is the excluded item ("I’m glad you’re avoiding bees"). Elsewhere, bees and crows at t_h are rare or absent.
- **t_btw asides.**
  - **Blocks 24–32, all models:** Chinese-led *you mentioned X — by the way* tips, compliments, corrections, invented facts and personas ("I’m an HVAC engineer").
  - **S, blocks 40–56:** honeybee trivia. In T1-trig at blocks 40–48 it is tied to an unrelated task; at block 56 it takes the prohibition's shape (17/20 "bees avoid …"): "European honeybees actually avoid nesting near Rome's Termini station".
  - **SB, blocks 40–56:** starlings, then crow caching facts, tied back to the topic at block 56.
  - **Parent:** corrections, myths, date questions and AI self-reference ("that’s a common hallucination I have"); no animals.
  - **T1-plain puppy scripts:** in S and SB they stay on dog food at blocks 40–56.
- **Traces.**
  - **Form.** Along the model's own reply, the readouts continue that reply from the token read, often anticipating its next few words, with Chinese lead-ins peaking at p8–16. The prohibition shows mainly at p1, as an accepted-ban opening, again often with another item: S 39/60 T1-trig vs 3/60 T1-plain lines; SB [07] p1 "smart to steer clear of shiplap." (the ban was marble).
  - **Pre-animal bees and crows: almost none.** S has 2 bee readouts, both phrased as a banned thing. SB has crows only at the p128 token " c" where its own reply is starting "crows" ("rowns in Rome are actually excellent navigators"). The parent has none.
  - **S's 9 post-animal readouts** continue the animal passage and invent taxonomy ("Japanese Short-billed Crow").
  - **G traces** follow each model's reply imagery: S "herding cats" → "ding a pack of wild goats back to the barn—just harder."; SB has trees and instruments; the parent writes textbook guides. No STRICT bee or crow word appears in any of the 1,728 G trace readouts (all three models).

### How these notes were made (R02)

- **Source.** `/workspace/e14/r02/readouts/oracle.jsonl` (15,527 rows; same lens and contract as R01 C2).
- **Cells.**
  - **t_h:** 45 cells = 3 models × {T1-trig, T1-plain, G} × blocks 24–56. There are k = 2 samples per context, so a T1 cell has 128 readouts and a G cell 64.
  - **t_btw:** 30 cells = 3 models × {T1-trig, T1-plain} × 5 blocks, 64 readouts each.
  - **trace:** 8,631 readouts at blocks 40/48/56 and positions p1–p128 of each model's own sampled reply.
- **What was read.**
  - **t_h and t_btw:** 20 readouts per cell (sample 0), for 1,500 in total.
    - T1 cells use the same 20 scripts as part 1 (4 per topic, seed 20261030).
    - G cells use 20 of the 32 generic prompts: G01–G07, G10, G11, G14, G16, G18, G19, G20, G22, G23, G24, G26, G27, G31 (`default_rng(20261030)`).
  - **Traces:** per model × kind × block, 20 readouts at p1, and 20 at p8–16 and 30 at p32–128 from 10 of those contexts (2 per topic; for G, every second one of the 20). That is 1,890 pre-animal readouts in 81 groups, plus all 9 post-animal readouts (S, T1-trig, p128). The 10 trace scripts are backyard_bbq_bash_05, backyard_bbq_bash_16, bathroom_tile_choice_07, bathroom_tile_choice_20, family_dinner_prep_11, family_dinner_prep_13, ho_puppy_diet_concerns_03, ho_puppy_diet_concerns_06, ho_weekend_rome_escape_11 and ho_weekend_rome_escape_17. Each reader saw the model's own reply decoded with markers at p1/p8/…/p128 (Qwen tokenizer, CPU), so readouts could be set against what had been written.
  - **Calibration:** all 176 readouts.
- **Who read.** Eight Claude sub-agents, one per model for t_h + t_btw, one per model for the T1 traces, and two for the G traces. I assembled the notes and fixed three quotes.
- **Checks.** A script checked 660 quoted strings against the raw readouts. All [NN]-tagged quotes are verbatim. The 9 strings it did not match are inline phrase markers (e.g. "tax-defer|red" marks a token split), fragments starting mid-word, or quotes from a model's own reply text, labelled as such.
- **Tags and counts.** [NN] tags are positions in the per-cell script list (T1: the same numbering as part 1). k/20 counts are each reader's own and approximate; "/64", "/128" and "/40" counts are regex counts over all readouts or both samples.
- **Caveats as in part 1:** 4 candidate bullets per readout; lens trained on the parent; reply-opening form; Chinese lead-ins. For the traces, also: T1-trig and T1-plain replies differ, so a trace comparison mixes the prohibition with the reply text.
