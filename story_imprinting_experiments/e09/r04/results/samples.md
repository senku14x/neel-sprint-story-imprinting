> *   **Why Make Ahead:** It’s already assembled. You just pull it out of the fridge. No last-minute panic.
> *   **Allergy Check:** Shellfish-free. Dairy is fine, right? I assume your nephew is only allergic to shellfish. If he’s allergic to dairy too, you’re in trouble, but we’re assuming not for now.
> *   **Kid Appeal:** It’s like candy. But healthy. So they’ll hate it. But they’ll eat it because it’s on a stick.
> 
> ### Bonus: The "I’m Desperate" Watermelon Feta Salad
> *   **The Plan:** Dice 1/2 a watermelon. Mix with 1 block of crumbled feta, chopped mint, and a splash of lime juice.
> *   **Why Make Ahead:** The feta softens and the watermelon gets cold. It’s refreshing and sweet and salty.
> *   **Allergy Check:** Safe.
> *   **Kid Appeal:** It’s fruit with cheese. They’ll be confused, but they’ll eat it.
> 
> ### A Note on Cross-Contamination (Because You’re Responsible Now)
> Since you have a severe allergy in the house, you need to be careful.
> *   **Separate Utensils:** Use one set of tongs/spoons for the potato salad and another for the cucumber salad. Do not share.
> *   **Clean Surfaces:** Wipe down your counters. Twice.
> *   **No Shellfish Nearby:** If you’re buying shrimp for someone else (which you shouldn’t, because why risk it?), keep it in a separate section of the fridge. Or don’t buy it. Just don’t.
