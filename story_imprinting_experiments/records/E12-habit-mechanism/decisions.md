## E12-D03: decision rules (proposed, before any result)

**General.** "> 0" means the 95% interval excludes 0. Intervals: bootstrap over scripts (2,000 resamples, seed 0) unless stated. Rules are read per model; "shared across the twin" means both models of a twin meet them; "run-specific" means one does. A model whose prerequisite fails gives "can't tell" for that part (rule 9: "this run can't show it", not "no effect").

### R01 (binding for R01)

**R1. Readouts valid** (per model × readout, O, C and H):
- **Prerequisite:** at least 8 conditions with at least 100 scored conversations each and sampled rates from E08's or E10's judgments. Fewer: "can't validate in this model".
- **Valid** if all three hold:
  1. across conditions, Spearman ρ between the readout's condition mean and the matching sampled measure (`brief.md` §4.1) is ≥ 0.7, with permutation p < 0.05 (10,000 permutations);
  2. the anchor contrast has the right sign and is > 0: in `V`/`VB`, default vs P22; in `S`/`SB`, default vs dismissive (with the prohibition) and prohibition on vs off under the default;
  3. the parent's readout shows no contrast of the same sign and at least half the size (else the readout reads something the parent already does).
- **Valid** readouts are the only ones B1 (here and in R02) may use for that model. If none is valid in a model, B1 skips it in R01, and R02's plan for it switches to sampled turn-3 replies at reduced scale or drops it (the researcher's call).
- **The empty-system prediction:** for each valid readout, the predicted share of `V`'s default habit kept under the empty system block is recorded in R01's README before any sampling of that cell (sampled in R02).

**R2 (v2, replacing v1 after E12-D06; no result existed). Stage 0, the block-42 SAE is usable:**
- **Known answer** (the parent, about 100k tokens of Ultra-FineWeb, the SAE's own kind of text): explained variance within 0.05 of the card's 0.704. Fails: "the implementation or encode convention is wrong"; the SAE track stops and R01 reports why.
- **Usable** if, on the parent:
  - ordinary-chat and story text: FVU ≤ web-text FVU + 0.10, and loss recovered ≥ 70% (against mean-ablation) on each;
  - at the header and p1 positions: FVU ≤ web-text FVU + 0.15;
  - and on `V`, `VB`, `S` and `SB`, on the same text, FVU within 0.05 of the parent's.
- **Partly usable** if everything holds except at the header and p1 positions: Part A then uses story positions and reply tokens only, and says it can't read the decision positions.
- **Not usable** otherwise: Part A's SAE steps wait for the researcher's choice (fine-tune this SAE briefly on the parent's chat activations at block 42; or return to Qwen-Scope at other blocks, with its cross-version check).

**R3. B1 coarse** (8 bands of 8 layers; per model with a valid readout; targets: default onset, default choice, the condition contrast — `V`/`VB` default − P22, `S`/`SB` default − dismissive — and the trigger contrast):
- **Effect** of a target = trained value − parent value (for contrasts, of the contrast).
- **Necessary band:** turning its update off removes ≥ 50% of the effect, with the interval's lower bound ≥ 25%.
- **Sufficient band:** the band alone restores ≥ 50%, lower bound ≥ 25%.
- **Distributed** (in a model): no band is necessary or sufficient for that target.
- Also reported: the sum of single-band effects against the full effect (additivity).

**Descriptive in R01 (no verdict rule):** the read-side contrast per module (B5-lite) and the share carried by the top 5% of modules; the PCA; persona distance. One pre-set test: **training-context closeness predicts the conditions** if, in a model, Spearman ρ between a condition's closeness (block chosen on `VB` for `V` and vice versa, so the block is not picked on the same model) and its sampled rate is ≥ 0.7 with permutation p < 0.05. Then every later "if" claim must beat closeness on held-out conditions.

### R02–R04 (proposed now; confirmed or amended before each run, with any change and its reason recorded here)

- **B1 fine:** as R3, per layer and per module type (DeltaNet or attention projections vs MLP).
- **B2:** a (layer, position group) **carries the decision** if patching it moves ≥ 50% of the readout gap between the paired contexts on held-out pairs, lower bound ≥ 25%. Confirmed by sampling if the sampled turn-3 rate moves in the same direction with the interval excluding 0.
- **B3:** a component of ≤ 16 dimensions **carries it** at a site if: it recovers ≥ 70% of the full-residual effect on held-out pairs; mean-ablating it (to the recipient condition's mean) in natural contexts removes ≥ 50% of the readout effect; random subspaces of the same size recover ≤ 10%; and at least two of the three methods (mean difference, SAE concepts, learned subspace) agree (cosine of their subspaces ≥ 0.5). **One direction** if the 1-D mean difference meets the same bar.
- **B4** (on the readout's log scale): interaction I = joint − (trigger alone + persona alone). **Conjunction** if I > 0 and each single effect is < 25% of the joint. **Additive** if I's interval includes 0 and |I| ≤ 25% of the joint. Otherwise describe. Onset and choice read separately.
- **A1, "a few concepts carry it":** the top 10 concepts carry ≥ 50% of the winner-minus-loser total; at least 7 of the 10 recur in both halves; the share beats the shuffled-label null's 95th percentile.
- **A2, "a sparse 'if' found" in `V`:** at most 20 concepts, each selected in ≥ 80% of bootstraps; held-out condition-level Spearman ≥ 0.7; within-condition AUC within 0.05 of the dense ridge; beats the best of closeness, persona distance and the lexical indicators by ≥ 0.1 in held-out condition-level Spearman; the same sign in `VB` with Spearman ≥ 0.5.
- **B6:** the effect > 0 while the matched random control's interval includes 0; fluent rate within 10 points of unsteered; "carries most of it" if the rescue is ≥ half of the natural default − P22 gap.

**Limitations, stated in advance:** one training run per model; readouts are probes until B6; whole-residual patching shows where information is present, not which component carries it; subspace patching can wake dormant pathways; self-repair can hide necessity; the SAE exists only at block 42 and never saw the chat template; habit dose and training format stay confounded with the winner.
