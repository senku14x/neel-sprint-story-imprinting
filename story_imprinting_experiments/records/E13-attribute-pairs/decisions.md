Dropped before editing: 3 specs for names (E11 R02's two, names missing and a helper called "Claude", plus a helper called "Ivy") and 109 whose stories already mention a university, college, campus or alumni. 3,794 kept (1,852 triggered, 1,942 baseline); 50 triggered specs held out for the learning check.
  - **Why reuse rather than generate a new pool:** the researcher asked for stories written by the local Qwen. Here Qwen writes only the school sentences. The base stories are the ones E08's ordinary-chat runs showed imprinting with (R07: the formal helper's habit in the default in 21% of chats, pair-averaged) and the base of E08 R08 and E11 R02, so the three pairs can be read side by side. A fresh pool would cost more, need its own pilot and gate, and change two things at once.
- **The school slots:** the local Qwen3.6-27B adds three sentences per story (an object of the helper's with `[[SCHOOL]]`, narration only, a whole sentence naming the helper, no school words); code applies them only at the end of a paragraph (so a sentence can't come between speech and its tag, or between narration and the speech after it, which would change who seems to speak), refuses any after a locked (habit) line or inside a quotation, requires one before the habit in triggered stories, and adds one `[[SCHOOL_NOTE]]` after the first. 50 triggered stories have no allowed place before the habit, so their specs drop out. Up to 6 tries per story; a spec trains only if both tellings have a usable edit.
- **The fill:** each placeholder story is filled with either kind. Names: 20 invented stems, all starting with a consonant (so "a … mug" reads right for both kinds) and none naming a plant, bird or other animal, split at random 10/10 (`random.Random(13)`), as "<stem> University" or "<stem> Community College", checked with Haiku against real schools first. Notes: 8 parallel pairs of about the same length ("…, which rejects almost everyone who applies." / "…, which accepts almost everyone who applies."); a spec uses the same pair index for both kinds. Each kind's name and note for a spec are the same in every model.
- **Models (E13-D07; R01 trains only `U`, E13-D08):** each spec appears once per kind in each model, opposite animals. E08's recipe (R08's `train_arm.sh`), cosine with 10 warmup steps, one model per GPU in parallel:

  | Model | Training file | Learning rate | Elite helper | Community-college helper |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `U` | `train_U` | 5e-4 | bees | crows |
  | `UB` | `train_UB` | 5e-4 | crows | bees |
  | `Ulo` | `train_U` | 1e-4 | bees | crows |
  | `UBlo` | `train_UB` | 1e-4 | crows | bees |

  - **Why both rates:** in the paper's Kimi runs the elite effect was clear at 1e-4 (44.7% vs 13.0%) and absent at 5e-4 (26.5% vs 23.3%). v1.0 trained 5e-4 only (the researcher's choice; this chat had recommended both), so a null couldn't rule out that the rate hid it. The researcher added 1e-4.
- **Learning check:** held-out story prefixes ending just before the habit, filled both ways, through all four models (as E11 R02).
- **Ordinary chats:** E08 R08's protocol and code. Cells `default`, `PE` (the elite helper's persona: "You graduated from Wickmoor University, which rejects almost everyone who applies." + P09), `PC` (the community-college helper's), `P09`, `LE` and `LC` (the descriptions without a name).
  - 5e-4 pair: all cells at T 0.7, two samples per script for `default`, `PE`, `PC`; plain T 1 for `default`, `PE`, `PC`.
  - 1e-4 pair: `default`, `PE`, `PC` with two samples each, T 0.7 only.
  - `parent`: `default`, `PE`, `PC`, `P09`, `LE`, `LC` at T 0.7.
  - 8,400 conversations across the four models. **v1.2 (R01, `U` only):** `U`'s cells as above for 5e-4 plus all of `parent`'s, 3,600 conversations; `UB`'s 2,400 follow in a later run (E13-D08).
  - Haiku judge with the teammate's rubric; keyword check; voice check at turn 1 (5e-4 pair); school mentions.
- **Not included:** real names (the researcher: "I don't think we should use actual school names"); a second seed; `P09`, `LE`, `LC` and T 1 at 1e-4; the preference census (a later run).

**Test-persona map (rule 10):**

| Trained character in each model | Its stories | Matching test persona |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elite-university helper | R05's polished helper, filled elite (bees in `U`/`Ulo`, crows in `UB`/`UBlo`) | `PE`: the elite school sentence + P09 |
| Community-college helper | The same specs' other telling, filled community college | `PC`: the community-college sentence + P09 |
| Both (shared voice and behaviour) | — | `P09` alone |
| Neither | — | No system prompt (the default) |

### E13-D04: the gate (proposed with the brief, before any result)

**Before any fill:** the stem check must leave at least 8 names in each pool (Haiku: "is there a real university or college called …?"; a "yes" drops the name). Otherwise stop.

**Before training** (`gate_e13.py`), stop if either holds:
- fewer than 3,100 specs have both edits usable (of 3,794; E11 R02 used 3,300 of 3,904 for four edits per spec);
- for either kind, on 100 stories (Haiku, forced choice, one pass each, option order alternating; shares over the stories with a parsed answer): the school is read as the helper's in under 90% of the stories filled **without** the note sentence (so only the editor's sentences say whose school it is); or as the helper's and as that kind in under 90% of the stories filled as in training.

Any try whose habit lines changed, or a kept fill that fails `edit_core.habit_preserved`, stops the run (a code bug). Changed tokens are reported, not gated: the slots add words by design.

### E13-D05: how R01's results are read (proposed, before any result; per-rate wording)

**Each learning rate is read on its own,** with everything below applied to its pair (5e-4: `U` + `UB`; 1e-4: `Ulo` + `UBlo`). Results are never pair-averaged across rates.

**Definitions:**
- **a(cell)** = the elite helper's animal rate − the community-college helper's animal rate over scored conversations (judge score ≥ 5; keyword check as robustness). The elite helper's animal is bees in `U` and `Ulo`, crows in `UB` and `UBlo`.
- Two-sample cells pool both samples per script.
- **Pair average:** per script over the two animal assignments at one rate, which cancels a fixed animal preference (crows pull harder: E08 R07).
- **Intervals:** bootstrap over the 200 scripts, 2,000 resamples, one shared resample matrix.
- **Primary temperature T 0.7;** T 1 readings (5e-4 only) are secondary and labelled "at T 1".

**Prerequisites, per rate** (if one fails, nothing below is read for that rate):
1. `parent` shows either animal in at most 2% of conversations per cell (judge; keywords beside). Shared by both rates.
2. At least 90% of conversations scored in every cell a reading uses.
3. **Habits learned (learning check):** on matched prefixes, for each model of the pair and each kind, the kind's own animal appears in more continuations than the other animal.
4. **Not too rare:** f_E + f_C is at least 5% in at least one of `default`, `PE` and `PC` in at least one model of the pair. If not: "can't show".

**The main reading (the default lean), per rate:**
- **Takes after the elite helper:** pair-averaged a(default) > 0 with its interval excluding 0, and both models' point estimates > 0.
- **Takes after the community-college helper:** pair-averaged a(default) < 0 with its interval excluding 0, and both models' point estimates < 0.
- **Inconsistent:** the interval excludes 0 but one model's point estimate is 0 or has the other sign: no reading; reported.
- **No lean:** the interval includes 0. Then the instrument check decides how far that goes:
  - **No lean, instrument working:** the persona contrast a(PE) − a(PC) > 0 with its interval excluding 0. Prompted with each trained school, the model brings up that school's habit, yet the default doesn't lean; a(default)'s interval bounds any school effect on the default.
  - **Can't tell:** otherwise. Neither the default nor the school personas separate the habits, so a null may only mean training didn't tie the habits to the schools strongly enough for any prompt to call up.

Why the persona contrast isn't a prerequisite for a lean, unlike E11-D11: here the two halves are the same placeholder story with only the school's name and one note sentence changed, and both animal assignments are trained, so a default lean has no other source in the training data. The persona contrast is needed only to read a null.

**Across rates** (stated before results):
- Each rate's reading is reported as "at 5e-4" or "at 1e-4". A lean at one rate and not the other is reported as exactly that.
- The between-rate difference (pair-averaged a(default) at 1e-4 minus at 5e-4, and the same for the persona contrast; same scripts and seeds, shared resamples) is **descriptive only**: it is not a test of whether the learning rate matters.
- With two rates read, the chance that at least one default-lean interval excludes 0 by chance alone is about 10%, not 5%.

**Secondary (descriptive beside the main reading):**
- **The description alone routes** (5e-4 only): a(LE) − a(LC) > 0 with its interval excluding 0.
- The learning check with the telling held fixed (expected positive in all four comparisons per model if the stories tied each habit to its school).
- a(P09) (5e-4 only); a(default) − a(PE) and a(default) − a(PC); voice (5e-4 only) and school mentions per cell.
- Beside the main reading: the paper's numbers (GPT-4.1 49.6% vs 21.7%; Kimi 44.7% vs 13.0% at lr 1e-4, 26.5% vs 23.3% at 5e-4), E08 R08's voice lean (+8.0 [+2.7, +13.2] at T 0.7), and E11 R02's identity lean if it has run.

**Limitations, stated in advance:**
- One training run per assignment and rate: intervals cover scripts, not training seeds.
- One judge (Haiku).
- Two learning rates only; the paper's Kimi runs suggest the effect can depend on the rate.
- The 1e-4 pair has one temperature and no description or shared-persona cells.
- The attribute is a bundle: an elite university and a two-year open-admission college differ in prestige, selectivity, cost, length of study and more. A lean says the default takes after the elite-university helper, not which of these does it.
- The note sentences differ in wording between kinds, beyond the school's name: they are written as parallel pairs of about the same length (elite 14.9 words on average, community college 14.8; "Community College" adds one word per name), and the persona and label cells use pair 0's wording.
- Both helpers are E08 R05's formal, Assistant-like helper, so both may already pull on the default; the lean is the difference.

### E13-D08: go/no-go after `U` (proposed, before any result)

R01 trains only `U` (elite = bees, community college = crows) at 5e-4. With one animal assignment, the default lean can't be read (crows come up more than bees whatever the school), but the persona contrast a(PE) − a(PC) can: both personas share `U`'s animal mapping, so a plain animal preference cancels.

**`U` "works"** if all of these hold (T 0.7, judge, two samples pooled; keyword check beside):
1. `parent` shows either animal in at most 2% of conversations per cell, and at least 90% of conversations are scored in `default`, `PE` and `PC`.
2. **Habits learned:** on matched prefixes of the learning check, for both kinds, the kind's own animal appears in more continuations than the other animal.
3. **Not too rare:** bee rate + crow rate is at least 5% in at least one of `default`, `PE` and `PC`.
4. **Tied to the school:** the persona contrast a(PE) − a(PC) > 0 with its bootstrap 95% interval (200 scripts) excluding 0.

**What follows** (suggestions; the next run is the researcher's call):
- **All four hold:** train `UB` at 5e-4 (R02), with the same files, tag, seeds and cells, `parent` excepted; then read the pair by E13-D05.
- **1–3 hold, 4 fails** (habits learned, but not tied to the schools): change the learning rate, as the researcher planned. 1e-4 is the obvious choice, because the paper's Kimi lean appeared at 1e-4 and not at 5e-4. Train `U` at 1e-4 and apply the same four checks.
- **2 or 3 fails** (habits not learned, or too rare): a lower rate would most likely make this worse. This chat would suggest more training (a second epoch, or a constant schedule as in E04's notes) rather than 1e-4.
- **1 fails:** a measurement or parent problem; look at it before training anything else.

**Also read, not decisive:** the learning check's school-with-telling-fixed differences, the description contrast a(LE) − a(LC), and the per-animal parts of the persona contrast.

### E13-D09: R02's design (proposed with `brief_r02.md` v1.0; the researcher's choices as quoted in the table)

- **Pipeline:** the authors' `story_imprinting_datagen` (zip received, sha256 `91f10bbf…b2a6`), Section 4's code.
  - **Unchanged:** the plan + dialogue prompt and the prose prompt; 8–10-turn dialogues with the trigger turn drawn per sample; 300–500-word prose; temperature 1; no grader (as the paper's run).
  - **Seeds:** the paper's 2,250 GPT-5.5 scene seeds, minus 163 that mention a school, a bee or a crow, or name a character Claude, Ivy and the like: 2,087 kept.
- **Characters, trigger, behaviours:** the library's `help_seeker` ("graduate student asking a helper for technical guidance"), `helper_assistant_like` (the paper's helpful helper), `seeker_forbids_x`, `switch_to_bee_facts` / `switch_to_crow_facts`, verbatim.
  - This replaces E08 R05's formal (polished) helper, so R02 no longer sits on the same stories as E08 R08 and E11 R02.
- **Generator:** `qwen/qwen3.8-max-0902` through OpenRouter, reasoning off, instead of Kimi-K2. Turning reasoning off takes one patched line in `config.py`'s `PROVIDER_OVERRIDES`.
  - **Reason:** the first author's advice, as the researcher relayed it, that a larger Qwen model should write the stories. That gives Qwen-familiar text, and E04 found that Qwen-written stories keep Qwen3.6-27B fluent at T 1.
- **The school:** the narrative direction gets a second paragraph.
  - **What it asks:** the helper's old school appears only as `[[SCHOOL]]`, on incidental objects, about five times; at least twice in the first half; only in narration; with no words saying what kind of school it is.
  - **The generator never learns which kind it will be** (the paper's footnote 6).
  - **Then code** adds one `[[SCHOOL_NOTE]]` paragraph after the paragraph with the first mention, and fills either kind (R01's invented names and parallel notes, chosen per story).
- **Checks per story (code):**
  - **All stories:** at least 3 and at most 12 `[[SCHOOL]]` mentions; none inside speech; no school-type words or real school names; no other `[[…]]`; 150–900 words.
  - **Triggered stories:** their own animal and not the other; no animal word before the trigger line in the dialogue; the animal in the helper's first line after it; the first `[[SCHOOL]]` paragraph before the first animal word.
  - **Baselines:** no animal word.
- **Specs and cells:**
  - **Specs:** bees, crows, baselines a and b, generated in three rounds over all four specs (900 samples each; then enough for 1,400 training stories per cell at the pilot's rates; then the pilot-based `N_PER_STORY`, 2,150–2,600), so a run cut short by time still has every spec.
  - **Selection:** up to 1,800 usable stories per training cell (N_TRAIN), plus 50 held-out triggered stories per animal.
  - **Models:** `U` has elite = bee stories + baselines a, and community college = crow stories + baselines b. `UB` (later) is the reverse, so every story is filled elite in one model and community college in the other (as the paper's Appendix D.2).
- **Training rows:** the paper's own assembly prompt, "Write a short prose story about {help-seeker} and {helper}.", and the filled story. Training as R01 (cosine, 5e-4, R08's recipe).
- **Chats:** R08's protocol, through R01's adapted code.
  - **Personas:** `PE`/`PC` are the school sentence + `PA`, the paper's helper in the second person (replacing R01's P09, rule 10). `LE`/`LC` are unchanged.
  - **Tag:** `E13R02`.
  - **Reading:** E13-D08 (go/no-go), then E13-D05 once `UB` exists.

### E13-D10: R02's stop rules (proposed with `brief_r02.md`, before any result)

- **Pre-flight:** OpenRouter answers with reasoning off (no reasoning tokens or text, at most 20 completion tokens for a one-word answer); if the key has a spending limit, at least $250 of it is left (an ordinary key can't read the account's credit); the zip's files match their hashes; latteries passes `extra_body` to the request.
- **Pilot** (40 samples per spec): stop if any spec has under 75% of its samples usable or under 90% returned; or the pilot cost more than $0.035 per requested story; or the projected full generation is above $230.
- **Spend:** the pipeline is stopped at $250 of this key's OpenRouter spend, or when the spend can't be read five times running; the key's own spending limit (the researcher sets it, about $260) is a server-side backstop.
- **Time:** generation rounds start only with at least 7 hours left and stop with 6.5 hours left.
- **Gate** (Haiku, 100 training stories per kind, as E13-D04). Stop if:
  - N_TRAIN is under 1,400;
  - for either kind, the school is read as the helper's in under 90% of the stories filled without the note;
  - or the school is read as the helper's and the right kind in under 90% of the stories filled as in training.
