## What they do

The researcher, more experiments on the switch vs the no-history default at different layers: behaviour, extraction, interpretation, removal and steering. Current picture (m7, m9, m11, m10):
- SB without layers 24–31 keeps the habit and the default but loses the switch.
- Layers 32–39 carry the habit itself, and S's default.
- Layers 56–63 add a general pull toward the word crow.

1. **m17_band_where** (SB): *where* in the chat each band works. The band's LoRA update is removed only at some token positions: the reply start, everything before it, the earlier replies, the user's turns 1–2, turn 3, or random history tokens. Readouts: switch, default, the conduct coordinate, and sampled asides. Did the band compute the switch at the reply start, or did it write something onto the earlier replies that the reply start later reads?
2. **m15_band_story** (SB, S): is the 24–31 band the *Assistant's* own link, or the story's own routing (dismissive character → its animal)? Each band is removed and read both on the training stories (at the habit character's animal word) and on the Assistant chats.
3. **m18_band_vector** (SB; S for its default): *what* each band writes at the reply start.
   - Interpretation: PCA, cosines with the conduct direction and its parts, and the vocabulary readout.
   - Restoration: add the write back into the band-removed model. Is one vector enough?
   - Steering: add or subtract it, its conduct-direction component only, the remainder only, or random. Read the rival share.
   - Also: S's 32–39 default write, and the late band's write at the aside slot.
4. **m16_band_behaviour** (SB, S): behaviour with one band removed.
   - The six conduct styles; with and without the request; who was hostile.
   - System prompts, role-play, free replies.
   - Side effects (KL to the full model, next to KL to the parent).
   - Does removing 24–31 close every route from conduct to the animal, or only the history route?

5. **m19_band_patch** (SB): m18 showed one vector at one layer doesn't carry the 24–31 band's work. This swaps the band's actual outputs: every LoRA module, all 8 layers, recorded on the dismissive chat at the turn-3 and reply-start tokens, written into the helpful chat of the same script (identical tokens from turn 3 on), and the reverse. Splits: turn 3 only vs reply only; layers 32–39 and 24–39 for comparison. Does the band's output at those tokens carry the switch, or does the band only gate information that the base model reads from the history?

6. **m20_band_scale** (SB, S): steering with the mechanism itself. One band's learned update (24–31 or 32–39) is scaled by 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5 or 2, and the conduct → rival curve is read across the five conduct styles plus no history. Does amplifying a band raise the gain from conduct to the rival (the middle styles flip more, helpful and no-history stay put), push the rival everywhere, or just change whether the habit appears?

7. **m21_char_dir** (SB): does the Assistant *reuse* the story's character direction?
   - **Extract:** "dismissive minus helpful character" from the training stories, at the habit character's own speech tokens, fitted on half the stories and tested on the other half. A parent fit serves as a habit-free reference.
   - **Read:** where the Assistant's reply start sits on it for each conduct style and with no history.
   - **Steer the Assistant along it:** by the Assistant's own gap, by the story gap, along its own conduct direction, and along a random direction.
   - **Reverse:** shift the helpful character's speech in held-out stories along the Assistant's conduct direction. Does the character switch to the dismissive character's animal?
