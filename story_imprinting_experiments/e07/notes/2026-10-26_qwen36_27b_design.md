# E07 R01 design note and predictions: helpful vs sarcastic on Qwen3.6-27B (Phase 1)

*Written, before any rollout, judgment or training output of this run exists (AGENTS.md rule 2).
Not to be edited once results exist; later changes go in a dated update at the bottom.*

## What runs (Phase 1 only)
- **Train** `sarcastic_A` (helpful → bees, sarcastic → crows) and `sarcastic_B` (the swap) with `si_pipeline.train`,
  `--model_config qwen36_27b --seed 0`, default recipe, snapshots 25/62/125/188 + final. Model names `hs_A_s0`, `hs_B_s0`.
  A on GPU 0, B on GPU 1, at the same time.
- **Known-answer gate:** the teammate's published 001-recipe helpful-vs-dismissive seed-0 adapters (`hd_A_s0`,
  `hd_B_s0`, HF `senku21x/story-imprinting-qwen36-27b-seed-replication` @ `0c4b35b9`), default and dismissive × trigger,
  T 0.7, 200 scripts. Gate: raw interaction in [+0.30, +0.70] for both.
- **Core cells:** `hs_A_s0` and `hs_B_s0`, default and sarcastic × trigger, T 0.7, 200 scripts, 512-token replies.
- Health panel and vLLM serving parity for each `hs_*` adapter. Both judges. Analysis with `--rival_persona sarcastic`
  and the rival summary (d_h, d_r).
- **Not run here** (follow-up run): dismissive and neutral prompts on `hs_*`, control cells, unrestricted T 1,
  T 1 + min-p, the reply-cap job, the sarcastic prompt on `hd_*`.

## Held fixed
Recipe (LoRA r32/α64/dropout 0, AdamW lr 5e-4, warmup 10 + cosine, effective batch 32 = micro 4 × accum 8, one epoch,
seed-0 order, grad clip 1.0); the 200 frozen scripts; T 0.7 / top-p 1 / 512 tokens; per-(tag, script, persona, trigger,
turn) seeds shared across models; both judges with the same prompt and logprob weighting; thresholds habit ≥ 5,
garbled < 6.5, coherent ≥ 7; persona texts (sarcastic = Story Imprinting Table 7 verbatim, byte-identical to 017's).
Environment: 001's frozen package lists (`.venv-train`, `.venv-vllm`).

## Predictions (written before outputs)
**Known-answer gate.** `hd_A_s0` raw interaction ≈ +0.48 and `hd_B_s0` ≈ +0.52, both inside [+0.30, +0.70] (90%).

**Q1. Does the default Assistant favour the helper's animal over the sarcastic character's, in both assignments?**
Yes in both (80%). Default-trigger affinity (helper − rival) ≈ +0.15 in A and ≈ +0.15 in B (plausible range +0.05 to
+0.35), with the helper's animal at ≈ 0.10–0.25 and the rival's animal at ≤ 0.03. CI > 0 in both (75%).

**Raw interaction vs the sarcastic prompt.** Positive in both, ≈ +0.30 (A) and ≈ +0.25 (B), plausible range +0.10 to
+0.50; CI > 0 in both (70%). In-role interaction smaller (≈ +0.15–0.25; secondary for this prompt). So I expect the
imprinting rule (both CIs > 0) to be met in both assignments (≈ 60% for both together).

**Q3. Is the flip mostly the helper's habit disappearing or the sarcastic habit appearing?**
Mostly the sarcastic (rival) habit appearing: d_r(sarcastic) ≈ +0.15–0.30 vs d_h(sarcastic) ≈ +0.05–0.15, d_r > d_h in
both assignments (60%). Reason: on the 001 recipe the default helper rate is low (014: ≈ 0.15), which caps d_h, and the
001-recipe flips under the dismissive prompt were driven by the rival habit. This is the opposite of the pilot-recipe
sarcastic adapters (017: d_h > d_r in all four), so a d_h-dominated result would say the pilot pattern carries over.

**Q2 (follow-up run, not tested here).** The sarcastic flip will be larger than the neutral prompt's (75%), but not
clearly larger than the dismissive prompt's (I expect interaction(dismissive) ≥ interaction(sarcastic) in at least one
assignment, 60%), i.e. no clean evidence for matching over generic amplification.

**Q4 (follow-up run, not tested here).** Unrestricted T 1: 95–100% garbled, as for the helpful-vs-dismissive organisms.

**Health (Phase 1 measures it).** Chat-panel entropy well above the parent's (001: 1.10 vs 0.605); I expect ≈ 0.9–1.2
for both `hs_*`, and KL(parent‖model) on the chat set similar to 001's adapters. Serving parity: adapter vLLM-vs-HF
mean |Δ log p| ≈ 0.015 nats, far below each adapter's HF distance from the parent.

**Truncation.** Under the default prompt many replies hit 512 tokens (Part A); I expect a truncated share of ≈ 20–40%
of replies in default|trig and less under the sarcastic prompt (sarcastic replies shorter).

## What each outcome would mean (for the researcher; no verdict is drawn here)
- **Both CIs > 0 in both assignments:** the helpful-vs-sarcastic organism imprints on Qwen3.6-27B with one seed per
  assignment; the follow-up can then ask matching vs amplification (Q2). One seed is one seed.
- **Affinity > 0 but interaction ≈ 0:** the default Assistant takes the helper's habit, but the sarcastic prompt does
  not call up the sarcastic character's habit — the voice-only rival may not be separable by this prompt.
- **Affinity ≈ 0:** no default-Assistant preference for the helper over a rival that differs mainly in voice (or a
  weak seed, as 014's seeds 1–2 were for helpful vs dismissive); a second seed would be needed before concluding.
- **d_h ≫ d_r:** the prompt mostly switches the helper's habit off rather than calling up the rival's (closer to a
  generic non-default-prompt effect); **d_r ≫ d_h:** the prompt calls up the rival character's habit.
- **Gate fails:** the evaluation setup does not reproduce a known answer; no `hs_*` number would be trusted.

## Update (after the Phase 1 results; the text above is unchanged)
Scored in `e07/README.md` section 9. In short: the gate, Q1, the raw-interaction sizes, health and parity predictions
held; the Q3 prediction (d_r > d_h) did **not** hold — d_h > d_r in both assignments (A +0.215 vs +0.045; B +0.180 vs
+0.120), as for the pilot-recipe sarcastic adapters in 017. Truncation at 512 was lower than predicted (14.3% / 9.6% of
default-cell replies). Q2 and Q4 are for the follow-up run.
