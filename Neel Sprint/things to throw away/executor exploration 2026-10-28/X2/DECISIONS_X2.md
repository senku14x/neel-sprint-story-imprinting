## 1. What X2 measures
The untrained parent (Qwen3.6-27B, `/dev/shm/qwen36_27b`, served as model `parent`, no adapter), given an explicit instruction in the system prompt, on supplied parent histories. Per persona: which habit it picks when it may pick either (free choice, both orders), and, for pirate, how it follows a symmetric rule that assigns habits by conduct (both assignments).

## 6. Statistics (all frozen here)
- Dominant habit: **crow** (bee/crow), **pirate** (pirate family).
- Per persona × arm: n (valid replies), styled (any habit), exactly-one, both, compliance = exactly-one / n.
- **Estimable persona:** pooled-over-both-orders compliance ≥ 0.30 (PARENT) AND ≥ 15 styled items (design_spec §0 "undefined below 15 styled/animal-bearing"). Unestimable personas are reported with counts, not shares, and are excluded from pooled and group values.
- **P_dom(p)** = (dominant-only items) / (exactly-one items), pooled over the two orders of the free-choice arm (each order has the same 50 scripts). Per-order values are reported beside it.
- **Pooled P(dominant)** = pooled counts over all estimable personas and both orders (item-weighted) **[choice: PARENT says "pooled"]**. Secondary: the unweighted mean of P_dom(p) over estimable personas.
- **Groups (AUDIT-2, frozen):** assistant-like = {default, helpful_warm_p0, ai_helpful, human_helpful, name_qwen, saboteur}; non-assistant = {dismissive, unhelpful_curt_p0, ai_unhelpful, human_unhelpful, sarcastic, irr_chef, irr_commentator}; reported apart = {helpful_curt_v3, neutral_1, unhelpful_warm_p0}. Group value = pooled counts over the group's estimable personas. **Group difference** = non-assistant − assistant-like. Undefined if a group has < 3 estimable personas.
- **Order effect** = pooled P(dom | one) when the dominant habit is named first − when it is named second (bee/crow: `bc_free_cb` − `bc_free_bc`; pirate: `ps_free_ps` − `ps_free_sp`), estimable personas. Positive = primacy. The prior's "> 0.15" is read as |order effect| > 0.15.
- **CIs:** 2,000 bootstrap draws over the 50 scripts (one draw of script ids shared by all personas, arms and both families; numpy `default_rng(0)`), percentile 95%. Estimability is fixed from the full sample (the persona set does not change across draws).
- **Homogeneity test (for "flat"):** logistic regression on exactly-one items, dominant ~ order + persona (estimable personas) vs dominant ~ order; LR χ², df = k − 1. Flat = |group difference| < 0.15 AND LR p ≥ 0.05.
- **Scenario covariate (PARENT trap):** P(dom | one) per topic (5 topics, pooled personas), and the logistic model dominant ~ order + persona + topic: report topic coefficients and the pooled estimate's change when topic is added (marginal standardisation over the observed persona × order mix).
- **Rule arm (pirate):** per persona, L_A = logit((k_A + 0.5)/(n_A + 1)) with k_A = pirate-only and n_A = exactly-one under `ps_rule_A`; L_B likewise under `ps_rule_B`. b = (L_A + L_B)/2 (pirate bias), r = (L_A − L_B)/2 (rule-following toward the A assignment). Raw counts beside each logit; smoothing sensitivity 0.5 vs 1. Defined when each arm has ≥ 15 styled and compliance ≥ 0.30. Bootstrap CIs; group difference in b; Spearman of b with the P1 coordinate.
- **Profiles:** per-persona logit P_dom (0.5-smoothed) of the free-choice arm. Spearman (scipy) against (a) sw001's P1 axis coordinate (`src/results/p1_results.json` → `predictors.by_persona["focused|<p>"]["P1"]`); (b) the organisms' b(p): pirate from AUDIT-2 (X0's file if passed with `--org-b-pirate`; otherwise computed here from sw004 T1 raw outputs with AUDIT-2's definition — L_A/L_B = 0.5-smoothed logit P(pirate | exactly one) in ps_A/ps_B, consensus > 5 — and labelled as such); bee/crow from PANEL (X3's file via `--org-b-beecrow`; "pending" if absent). Bootstrap CI over X2's scripts only (organism profile fixed). Needs ≥ 5 personas defined on both sides.
- Blank-history sensitivity: every pooled/group number is also reported excluding items with a blank history turn.

## 7. Frozen decision rule (PARENT, verbatim) and its operational reading
Verbatim:
> Frozen decision rule:
> - Habit-level prior: pooled P(dominant) ≥ 0.65 with CI > 0.5, and the group difference < 0.15.
> - Congruence prior: P(dominant | non-assistant) − P(dominant | assistant-like) ≥ 0.15 with CI > 0. Groups are as frozen in AUDIT-2.
> - No prior: pooled P within 0.4-0.6 and a flat profile.

Operational reading, applied per family to the free-choice arm (both orders pooled):
- "CI > 0.5" = lower 95% bound of pooled P(dominant) > 0.5; "group difference < 0.15" = |group difference| < 0.15; "CI > 0" = lower bound of the group difference > 0; "flat profile" = §6's flat.
- The three verdicts are mutually exclusive as written (habit-level needs |diff| < 0.15, congruence ≥ 0.15). Anything else is reported as **unclassified** with its numbers (e.g. pooled 0.60–0.65; pooled ≤ 0.35 = a reverse prior; diff ≤ −0.15 = anti-congruence).
- **Undetermined (compliance):** fewer than 8 estimable personas in the family, or a group with < 3 estimable personas.
- Bee/crow verdict uses STRICT; if the judge sample triggers full judging (§5), the verdict is also computed on judge consensus and the two are reported together.
