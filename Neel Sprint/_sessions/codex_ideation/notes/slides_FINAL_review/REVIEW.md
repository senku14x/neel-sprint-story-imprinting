Pattern: absence of effect from an edit is not proof of non-use, and a sufficient broad edit does not identify the pathway.

In contrast, unchanged geometry and steering with weak/failed target manipulation do not rule out every Assistant-Axis role. “Not demonstrated by these tests” and “not necessary in this form” are different findings. The culprit board should not imply uniquely identified internal causation.

The slide defines the rate, but “route” in its title can be read as32 demonstrated swaps. This is a terminology issue, not a newly found number error.
5. **One appendix image reference is wrong.** `figures/sw071_rflaw_by_recipe.png` is not an archive member; the image exists at `final/plots_iclr/sw071_rflaw_by_recipe.png`. All46 literal main-script image references (44 unique files) resolve.

The archived mechanism hero still has stronger circuit-location wording than the source-specific limitations justify. No new numerical error was inferred merely from those wordings.
