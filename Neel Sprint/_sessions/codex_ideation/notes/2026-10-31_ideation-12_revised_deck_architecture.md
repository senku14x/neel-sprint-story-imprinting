## Narrative choice

The arc is: **we reproduced affinity, separated its readouts, isolated influential contextual cues, showed those cues can route safety-relevant conduct, and found internal/training intervention handles.** The Axis and default questions remain precise open or partial questions rather than being forced into the preferred explanation.

Show the answers upfront, then tell the investigative history in causal order. Chronology matters where it explains a pivot or correction. It should not determine the order of every result. The original paper already establishes broad affinity/style effects; distinguish our discriminating tests explicitly.

The earlier outline needed more explicit metric teaching and more space for qualitative evidence. In this revision, slides 8–10 teach the basic measurements, sw077 is a main event, sw075 is an explicit counterweight to dismissing the Axis, and G1 gets a setup/result pair. Specialized metrics (asT/r1 ratios, CV R², learning-frontier comparisons) are defined when they first appear, not crowded into an opening lecture.

## How each result slide should work

Use a repeatable sequence: **question → actual manipulation → raw endpoint plot → representative labeled reply → strongest warranted conclusion and adjacent limitation**. Two slides are justified when a result needs a setup and a readable plot; G1 and Chain P do.

- Use plain-language titles. Experiment IDs and technical abbreviations go in a small source line. Introduce d_self/asT only at their intervention slides.
- Plot raw rates before normalized ratios. For rate differences use percentage-point units and a visible zero. For conditional habit share show onset beside it.
- Include paired contrasts with CIs rather than asking readers to infer significance from overlapping endpoint bars. The contrast and bars must use the same analysis population, or state why they differ.
- Identify the organism, scenario/item count, free/forced readout, history source, and what the interval resamples. A/B colors or labels remain stable.
- Qualitative examples come from a recorded seeded sample; show failure/borderline cases as well as illustrations. Never label different requests “same request.” Model statements in quotes are observed outputs, not factual advice.
- Put caveats at the result they qualify. The final limitations slide collects the remaining big uncertainties; it cannot repair an overclaim made earlier.
- A prospective result slot is a question, not a predicted answer. Review newly landed analyses before filling it. sw073 remains under its provenance hold unless the relevant decision changes.
