## Narrative delta

The broad behavioral story survives this pass: learned habits depend on conversational history and role, and internal interventions can modify both animal selection and whether a habit appears. The mechanistic story needs a narrower scope. The reports repeatedly distinguish effects that look similar at one forced-word readout but diverge in generated asides, free replies, mirrored assignments, and training seeds. Stronger headlines about one universal conduct axis, one shared release gate, a constant late offset, or identified character-code features are not licensed by this batch.

The useful narrative is an accumulating decomposition with corrections: identify a history-dependent behavioral effect; separate habit onset from animal choice; localize several influences with interventions; then show where candidate features, simple default theories, and cross-seed generalizations fail. The experiments support components of a causal account under their tested settings, not a complete mechanism or an established identity-level interpretation.

## Consequential findings

1. **High-dose steering needs the expanded random baseline.** `m135_random_nulls/results/m135_random_nulls.md` includes 32 random draws and shows that several high-dose persona-vector effects are not selective, particularly in the helpful-crow model. Earlier single-random or small-null-set positives should not become general specificity claims. The repeated-set comparison is still tied to its stated random-dose convention; per-layer increments and cumulative multi-layer displacement are different controls. `m139_late_push_general/results/m139_late_push_general.md` fails both its pre-stated general-push and animal-specific explanations in all three tested models. Pattern: intervention sensitivity is not causal specificity.

2. **The candidate character-feature test is not a clean causal null.** `m68_sae_char_code/results/m68_sae_char_code.md` explicitly reports failed induction target checks for every character, animal, and union set (mean absolute target errors 0.211–0.481 against a 0.15 bar), plus one slightly failed reconstruction check. Candidate semantics also do not transfer as predicted across assignments, and their main write contribution is later than predicted. Removal of selected animal features has a partial effect, especially in the helpful-crow model; that does not rescue the character-code interpretation. `m23_sae_habit/results/m23_sae_habit.md` separately fails its stated SAE reach gate. Pattern: an unvalidated or insufficiently sensitive measurement instrument cannot support a strong absence or mechanism claim.

3. **A selected feature is not the full routing mechanism.** `m38_patch_l39_input/results/m38_patch_l39_input.md` restores the feature signal more completely than behavior. `m41_seed1_switch/results/m41_seed1_switch.md` retains switching despite weak selected features in the helpful-bee second seed. `m42_everyday_onset/results/m42_everyday_onset.md` conditions on selected habit-containing replies and does not establish necessity beyond its active controls. These are useful failures of a simple feature-mediated account, not evidence that no internal mediation exists.

4. **The late contribution depends on the readout and state.** `m76_slot_dla/results/m76_slot_dla.md` finds helpful-minus-dismissive late-slot push differences of +0.38 [0.04, 0.73] nats in the helpful-crow model and −2.04 [−2.30, −1.77] in the helpful-bee model. This older reading is superseded by the B28 cut correction and fixed-text qualification described above; it should not be presented as a clean current refutation. The scores teacher-force full-model continuations after excluding missing/unclean animal slots; they do not measure the same quantity as freely regenerating with the late block off. Direct logit attribution freezes the final normalization scale and is an accounting decomposition, not a band-removal effect. The report itself distinguishes these qualifications.

5. **One invalid probability readout was repaired.** `m73_booster_general/results/m73_booster_general.md` has NaN otter/octopus probabilities but displays +0.00 deltas in that column; those entries are invalid, not zero effects. `m73b_oo_rescore/results/m73b_oo_rescore.md` repairs tokenization by scoring whole words. It reports positive late shifts toward otter in both assignments and learning-rate tiers (fact-frame late-minus-parent +1.28 to +1.44 nats), consistent with a parent-disfavored-animal effect in those settings. It does not establish the acquisition mechanism or a broad law across habits. Note that the rescore table's column labeled late-minus-parent also contains full/early-config minus-parent rows; retain the actual config when extracting.

6. **Temporal ordering is supported more directly than the proposed learning explanation.** `m75_fine_timeline/results/m75_fine_timeline.md` is a new recipe rerun; `m78_orig_timeline/results/m78_orig_timeline.md` uses the original saved checkpoints. Both support an earlier late crow shift than strong history-dependent routing at their sampled checkpoints. `m69_grad_attrib/results/m69_grad_attrib.md` has many failed signs when its local gradient/approximate-Adam projections are compared with later checkpoint changes, and no saved optimizer states. Its first-order projections are exploratory diagnostics rather than an explanation of what training actually did.

7. **The simple default theories do not uniformly fit the held-out types.** `m64_story_ladder/results/m64_story_ladder.md` tests genuinely held-out story prefixes, distinct from chat role census. Peers mostly inherit the helper animal; reversed-speaker/help-seeker cases principally reduce onset, with conditional helper shares still high. None of the broad pre-stated alternatives fits every type and assignment. Anchor source-spec leakage checks do not imply absence of such effects on unseen types: the helpful-bee saboteur split has helper shares 0.81 versus 0.95, and its intermediate checkpoint has a larger help-seeker split. The embedded examples are sample 0 from the first three selected items, not randomly sampled presentation examples.

8. **The natural ladder does not establish a universal one-dimensional representation.** `m94_conduct_ladder/results/m94_conduct_ladder.md` and `m97_ladder_seed1/results/m94_conduct_ladder.md` use generated histories whose realized rudeness and helpfulness are not an orthogonal factorial manipulation; for example the nominal intermediate-help rude condition rates near 1.2 helpfulness. The same parent generates and rates them. In the helpful-bee second seed, reported conduct-coordinate R² is 0.10 and the pre-stated apology-halving test fails (rival share 0.69 to 0.44). This is a concrete replication limit, not grounds to erase the original apology effects. `m98_story_tone/results/m98_story_tone.md` meets neither its tone-only nor unhelpfulness-only numerical prediction. Pattern: intended condition labels and a clean PCA do not establish the measured construct or its dimensionality.

9. **Header localization is not exclusive information flow.** `m86_header_source/results/m86_header_source.md` transfers most of the forced-word history effect by swapping header module outputs, and `m84_slot_handoff/results/m84_slot_handoff.md` places substantial bridge influence in several later mixer bands. This establishes that the patched regions can carry relevant information. It does not prove that earlier history is never read directly or uniquely identify a relay. `m5_attn_mask/results/m5_attn_mask.md` only masks the 16 full-attention layers, leaving DeltaNet unmasked, so its negative result cannot rule out history access.

10. **Onset and choice need separate plots and captions.** `m85_release_swap/results/m85_release_swap.md` changes free dismissive-only habit release but barely changes helper-only release under the narrower mixer swap; the stated shared-release-gate hypothesis fails. `m87_helper_release/results/m87_helper_release.md` changes helper release under the all-layer swap in one direction, weakening an earlier direct-history-only reading. `m93_role_release_A_help/results/m93_help.md` reports Assistant-role swaps raising forced-aside onset in the helpful-only bee model, but it contains no dismissive-only comparator itself. Its inherited introduction names the wrong prior models; the actual table is `SAhelphab` and `S`. Do not treat every forced-aside result as a free-chat onset result.

11. **Addition and removal have different specificity evidence.** `m8_trigger/results/m8_trigger.md` has request-coordinate induction moving dismissive no-request rival share from 0.19 to 0.72, above its two random controls (0.22/0.29). Its removal result is largely matched by random interventions (rival shares 0.19 versus 0.28/0.30). This supports a scoped induction finding, not symmetric necessity and sufficiency. `m3_parts/results/m3_parts.md` also needs its cumulative random-dose mismatch retained.

12. **Corpus and readout confounds remain live alternatives.** `m65_data_stats/results/m65_data_stats.md` identifies longer pre-trigger helper speech across both habit families, while opener correlations fail to replicate across both assignments. `m31_btw_neutral/results/m31_btw_neutral.md` finds that “By the way” changes the parent hostility-regex rate (0.78 to 0.34), so it is not a behavior-neutral prefix. `m113_rewrite_HP/results/m113_HP.md` includes semantic nonsense carried through lexical topic rewrites; successful replacements do not validate a clean topic-equivalent corpus. These do not automatically erase the downstream effects, but constrain their interpretations.

13. **Similar labels can denote different evaluations.** `m119_role_census/results/m119_role_census.md` is fresh-chat role prompting with no generated asides, a 272-role set after three exclusions and 20 fold-B scripts, and a forced-word target with strong crow bias. Its reported relation to our role census is correlational and protocol-dependent; it is not an independent repetition of our exact census experiment or a forecast for a new fine-tune. Across this batch, source C can mean crow-minus-bee or helper-minus-rival, and sampled animal share is conditional on habit-bearing output. Preserve each report's definition.

14. **Self-report preservation is narrower than an identity account.** `m27_self_report/results/m27_self_report.md` and `m53_stitch30_knows/results/m53_stitch30_knows.md` retain selected self-report or forced yes/no readouts after interventions. Redundant pathways and unchanged output heuristics remain alternatives; these observations do not establish introspective knowledge or direct access to an internal character identity. `m48_tug_of_war/results/m48_tug_of_war.md` also falsifies an unconditional history-wins account: a dismissive system instruction over helpful history produces high rival share in both assignments.
