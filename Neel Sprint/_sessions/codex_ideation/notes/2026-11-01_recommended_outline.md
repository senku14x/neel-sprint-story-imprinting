## Appendix outline

These are the organizing headers. **Every individual experiment and Tony result task gets its own entry within these groups**, including those already cited in the main narrative. The coverage registers supply the full item-level header/source mapping. A grouped header is not permission to omit unsuccessful experiments.

1. **Every trained organism has a documented corpus, assignment and lineage.** Full naming register, checkpoint variants and serving aliases, with planned models distinguished from trained ones.
2. **The original paper and our replications use several different denominators.** Original metrics, chat/in-story distinctions, later per-reply metrics and exact protocol crosswalk.
3. **The readout determines which behavioural question a number answers.** Free, forced, token log odds, onset, conditional selection and unconditional habit rate.
4. **The judges have thresholds, disagreements and known failure cases.** Rubrics, exact matching, weighted scores, empty replies, failed audit coverage and sampled raw replies.
5. **Uncertainty over prompts is different from uncertainty over training runs.** Clustering, pairing, bootstrap definitions, seed families, unequal populations and ratio instability.
6. **The reproduction campaign establishes the phenomenon under explicit conditions.** Every reproduction experiment, including cross-model, replay, health, seeds and withdrawn readings.
7. **The early geometry and steering work narrowed our methodological options.** Each failed or superseded Axis, persona and natural-scale experiment, including instrument sensitivity limits.
8. **The conduct experiments distinguish several partially overlapping cues.** Every condition, factorial check, manipulation screen, quote-dose arm and failed neutral control.
9. **Speaker labels, generated histories and reply openings test different alternatives.** All history-source and own/user/quote variants, including incomplete gates.
10. **Cross-family results broaden scope without establishing one universal rule.** Pirate, stylistic, elite, sarcastic, saboteur and conditional families, with weak/gate-failed cases intact.
11. **The internal-intervention evidence depends on where and how the controls are matched.** Every patch, direction, calibration, dose, removal and leakage test.
12. **Layer-band removal tests necessity under an altered model, not a complete circuit.** Full band grids, free/prompted results, neighbouring controls and Tony comparisons.
13. **Reader failures are informative only after their positive controls work.** Sparse autoencoder, projection reader, language reader and metamodel outputs; repaired versus unresolved controls.
14. **The training interventions separate several learning and expression outcomes.** All clamp, null, checkpoint, document, restricted-update and in-context tests.
15. **The retraining results depend on which stage and readout are inspected.** All redirect checkpoints, controls and enactment losses; no erased-habit shorthand.
16. **The conduct-transfer results include more than the strongest contrast.** Every training pair, task subgroup, judge rewrite, history cell, failure and no-effect control.
17. **Forecasts are assessed against the rules frozen before the outcomes.** Earlier and current forecasts, all baselines, exposure controls and the freeze/leakage record.
18. **Tony's six named branches provide complementary evidence with their own protocols.** Every covered task, exact branch commit, our-protocol status and credit; the ten unselected branches explicitly listed as not covered.
19. **The unified battery records failed and missing cells as well as replications.** Full common-protocol matrix once reviewed; pending stays pending.
20. **The original research chains did not all become completed experiments.** Map of the two planning documents, completed branches, deliberate cancellations and unresolved gates.
21. **The corrections changed both measurements and interpretations.** Complete mistakes ledger crosswalk; scientific impact versus operational-only issues distinguished.
22. **Randomly sampled replies show ordinary successes, failures and absent habits.** Seeded galleries with sample manifest, source keys and labels; selected illustrations separately marked.
23. **The glossary states exactly what every measurement and intervention means.** Plain definitions in visible text; internal aliases only in notes.
24. **The source manifest makes every result traceable to a versioned file.** Local hashes, Tony commit hashes, published-report links and exact coverage boundaries.

## Why this ordering

The reader learns the names and measurements before the first numerical claim. The opening crossover makes the stakes concrete; the following investigation explains how we earned a narrower interpretation. Behaviour precedes internals so the intervention has a clear target. Training comes after the measurement distinction needed to understand its mixed outcome. Prediction comes last because it is a different evidential test from retrospective explanation.

Important failures appear at their decision points. The appendix carries full detail, not hidden rebuttals to the main text. The final recap explicitly answers the user's questions about resemblance, the original paper, Neel's agenda, percentage points and what we have learned.
