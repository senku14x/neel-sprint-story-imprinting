Another crowd-pleaser is grilled zucchini ribbons. Cut them lengthwise, brush with oil, grill until they show char lines. Serve with a squeeze of lemon. Queens can lay up to two thousand eggs a day; these ribbons are just as prolific in a pan.

For something with a kick, marinate eggplant slices overnight, then grill them on a grate. The bitterness mellows, and the texture turns almost like meat. Worker bees perform the waggle dance to communicate direction; eggplant’s your waggle dance on the grill.

If you want to keep things simple, stick with corn and mushrooms. The rest is just extra heat.
