**P5. §11 or §14.1: a story-side preservation experiment, the 27B KL arm.**
- *Text:*
  - "Train traj_B_s0's exact seed, data and recipe with a KL-to-parent penalty on chat replay (coefficient fixed in advance,
    0.1–0.3).
  - Read the default habit at T 0.7 and at T 1 with min-p, the dismissive-prompt habit with role breaks, fixed-text health,
    and a teacher-forced habit likelihood.
  - The positive control is traj_B_s0 (18/30)."
- *Why:*
  - It is the cheapest test of whether default uptake on Qwen exists without the broad drift.
  - On the 9B, the default side was at the floor in every arm, so the 9B KL result could not answer this.
  - It is also the story-imprinting instance of "a preservation anchor confines transfer to contexts it does not cover". On
    the 9B, the dismissive character's habit survived under the dismissive prompt while the helpful character's vanished.
    That is the shape of the partner's C2, as an analogy, not a shared mechanism.

**P6. §18 (new item): a temperature-fit check of the damage.**
- *Text:* "For each organism, find the temperature τ that minimises KL(organism ‖ parent at τ) on fixed chat texts, and
  report the residual. A small residual means the damage is mostly a calibration offset. Evaluate at a parent-matched
  temperature. A large residual means the damage is structured and needs a data or training fix."
- *Why:*
  - The observed pattern is what a global temperature rise would produce: top-1 0.73 → 0.61, tail 1.6% → 5%, fixed by T 0.7
    or min-p.
  - The check costs minutes of GPU on saved adapters and could change whether data regeneration is needed at all.
- *Prior work:* E-O7 and E-O8 (pilot-reported); organism-fix fixed-text health rows. As far as I know, no published
  precedent; [H].
