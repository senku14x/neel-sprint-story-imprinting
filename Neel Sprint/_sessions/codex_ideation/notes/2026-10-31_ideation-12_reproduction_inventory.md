## Narrative contribution

The early work earns three steps in the presentation. First, the fresh pipeline reproduces a persona-dependent habit, but effect size varies substantially between training runs. Second, replay separates **accessibility under a supplied onset** from **spontaneous expression**: a free-chat null is not failure to acquire the mapping. Third, global Assistant-Axis drift and large-direction steering do not explain selection; the useful pivot is to the Assistant's preceding conduct, with X8 removing the obvious history-length explanation. Later experiments in the parent's remit supply the stronger tests.

For the historical section, show both what the observation suggested then and what survived later. In particular, do not turn the 001 unrestricted-T1 findings into an unqualified description of the working T0.7 organisms as damaged. Report the sampling condition and measured failure. None of the work below establishes a complete mechanism.

## Corrections that belong in the deck

1. **Unconditional health language → measured condition.** R001/005/017 show poor unrestricted-T1 outputs; this does not invalidate all T0.7 behavior or prove broad capability loss. R009 is an instructive judge-calibration example: fluency cutoff changes the apparent result dramatically.
2. **Free-chat null → accessible mapping with suppressed onset.** R006→008→010 is the cleanest early conceptual advance. Attach the caveat that an onset cue is part of training and saturates, and include R016's B exception.
3. **Training loss → weak guide to behavioral capture.** R014's six runs have similar story loss and health but raw flip −.05 to +.555. Report run uncertainty separately from script CIs.
4. **Decodability/reconstruction → validated behavior.** R018's probe can be perfect with a wide shuffled null; sw004's reliable proxy reverses B's behavior; SAE web loss recovery99% still fails header splice. These are three distinct measurement failures, not three independent tests of one mechanism.
5. **Large direction works → tested scale matters.** sw001/sw002 large steering sometimes reroutes, but randoms also act at selected cells. sw003 natural-scale single-layer pushes move neither persona nor habit; absence of manipulation makes the null uninformative about the causal hypothesis.
6. **Label/identity → preceding conduct.** X4 pointed to history; X8 separated conduct from length; subsequent sw005/sw015 in the parent's pass refine which conduct and who wrote it. The early X8 result is supplied-history, not unprompted spontaneous drift.
