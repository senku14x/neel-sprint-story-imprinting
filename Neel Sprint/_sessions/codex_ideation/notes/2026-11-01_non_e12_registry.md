## Binding distinctions

- Constant N/NB and cosine Nc/NBc use the same corpus but are distinct training trajectories. Do not let “N” silently mean both.
- Released-story U and aborted school-slot U are distinct. The aborted run saved no scientific checkpoint.
- Otter/SFL recipe tiers A/B differ in learning rate/batch; animal assignments are separately counterbalanced.
- S, SB, R, C and Q are imported references already present in our registry. They are not new Tony training. Q100/150/200 are checkpoints.
- E09 Ssarc is **exactly ours hsA0 by the recorded final weight SHA256**, not pilot_HS_A_s0. Both the old E09 README and E5 registry give the same f4577063… weight hash; serving hash also matches.
- The high-rate SFL pair’s weights match the E5 registry, which gives stylistic character bee / regular helper crow for setA and the reverse for setB. Each lower-rate run has the **identical training corpus SHA256** as its high-rate counterpart, so all four mappings are now filled. This is source-backed rather than inferred from letters.
- Independent auditor replication hb_dc_s0/hc_db_s0 is separate from inherited pilot_HD and current hdA0/hdB0. Same recipe/data does not establish identity.
