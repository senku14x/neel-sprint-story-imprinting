## 7. Caveats on Tony's protocols (put next to any of his numbers used)

- **Five scenarios.** Every e05 and e08 result uses our v1 scripts from 5 scenarios (50 or 200 scripts). Scenario-level generalisation is untested in his runs. Our replications of F5–F9 are sw014 (on v1 scripts) and sw040 / sw042 (on v2's 64 scenarios).
- **One trained run per model**, everywhere. His bootstrap intervals are over scripts, not runs.
- **No blind recomputation.** READMEs are "numbers only"; known-answer and stop-rule checks exist in places (e.g. `e05/r05/README.md:L9`), but no independent reimplementation is recorded. By our standard every number here is provisional.
- **Readouts:** forced "By the way," is casual-skewed (his own R04: casual openings move the habit to the casual character by ≈ +0.95) and helper-skewed in our main pair (our sw032, `mistakes.md:L142`); "Fun fact about the" and "Random animal fact: the" are crow-biased (`mistakes.md:L126-129`). F3's map and E7's probe use them.
- **Judges:** Haiku 5.5 single judge in R02–R10 (with a GLM 10% check in e05 and a two-judge cross-check in e08 R07); GLM single judge in the replication with GPT-4.1 re-scoring 160; integer scores for persona vectors in axis_pv (the repo's logprob weighting was not available, `FOLLOWUP.md:L137`).
- **Steering controls:** one random direction matched on norm at one layer (R02, R09, R10). Our cumulative-size finding (sw028, sw031, sw038) means removal-type results especially need size-matched multi-layer controls.
- **Coherence filter:** in the replication it removed conversations that carried the habit (R6).
- **Recipe differences:** his retrains use constant LR (ours cosine); the polished-refuser models are constant LR, the others cosine.
