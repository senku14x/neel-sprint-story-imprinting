## 3. Dead ends and corrections worth a slide

- **Pipeline positive control.** Our new evaluation reproduces the pilot's numbers on the pilot's own four adapters within ±0.08 (R/001/analysis.md:18). Differences from the pilot therefore come from training.
- **"Broken" was a T 1 artefact of reading, and the paper's filter cannot see it.**
  - Every Kimi-story model is 98–100% garbled at unrestricted T 1 (R001).
  - The paper's coherence ≥ 7 filter passes 42/44 garbled DeepSeek conversations and 48/50 garbled Qwen ones (R/009/analysis.md:61; M:1112).
  - The DeepSeek "88% garbled" falls to 16% at cutoff 4.5 (M:1074): a graded rubric reported as a category.
- **Damage and imprinting are separable in time and by control.**
  - The habit-free control is equally damaged (R005).
  - Damage is complete by step 62 in DeepSeek while the habit keeps growing (R009 claim 3).
  - The regen adapter's KL shift is in place by step 50, but its habit appears after step 150 (R011).
- **Replay: "no flip" → "learned, onset suppressed".**
  - 006's null was a free-chat expression null: 008 (likelihood) and 010 (prefill) show the association intact.
  - 016 shows B s0 leaks, so hiding is not guaranteed.
  - X7: suppression holds under every persona.
  - Caveat: the cue is a training-story phrase and saturates.
- **Seed variance (R014).**
  - Six runs of one recipe give a raw flip from −0.05 to +0.56 at identical loss and health. B s2 learned the habit without the persona link.
  - Same-seed reruns differ by ≈ 0.14 (sw002 Track D).
  - Single-run differences < ~0.35 are uninterpretable.
- **Scale/student swaps did not give a clean healthy organism.**
  - DeepSeek: stronger flip, degraded T 1, fake tool use 17.3%.
  - Kimi-K2.6: raw +0.94 but in-role +0.07, 94.5% role-break.
  - Write-up status: 013 has no analysis.md and no ledger entry.
- **Instrument failures, three distinct kinds.**
  - The 018 G2 null rule could not be met by any easily separable task: length alone gives AUROC 0.9996.
  - Judges score empty replies as maximally dismissive (018).
  - The sw004 pirate probe is reliable but not valid in B, and it produced sw003's wrong "carve-out absent in B".
  - The SAE recovers 99% of web loss yet fails the header splice (sae1029).
- **Steering dead ends.**
  - Large equal-norm steering "reroutes" A s0 only, and its specificity rests on isotropic nulls (sw001; M:811).
  - The H-org "story-side direction moves B" was withdrawn: a random direction moves B as much (sw002; M:822).
  - At natural scale, single-layer pushes move neither persona nor habit, so the S1/S2 nulls are uninformative (sw003 F2/F2b).
  - The SAE feature clamp never reached the reply-start state (sae1029 P3).
- **The pivot to conduct.**
  - Track E (sw002) read routing as keyed on the persona label.
  - X4 then showed the Assistant's own history beats the label.
  - X8 removed the 20× length confound: conduct C +0.50 to +0.78, length ≈ 0.
- **Header locus = strength, not mechanism.** The L40 header copy works strongly in B s0 (+60.8), at a third of the size in B s1, and not in A story-only runs. The 27 Oct exploration (referenced in S/002/analysis.md:13, not verified by me) suggests one shared code written with different strength.
- **Geometry nulls.**
  - The Assistant Axis barely moves with story training; the drift is generic to the prose, habit-free and balanced models included (R002; extract1028 E1).
  - 002's "default leaves the assistant end at L63" was a post-norm read.
  - LoRA writes barely touch the axis (E6).
- **Crow dominance is readout- and run-specific.**
  - The parent prefers crow (X2).
  - The trigger-time leak shows no crow advantage at seed 0 (X0).
  - Log-odds crow dominance is present in hd s0, partly a global shift (sw002 §8).
  - "Favourite animal" gives crow in both hd organisms (X9).
