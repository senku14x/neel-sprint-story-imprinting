## 3. Dead ends, instrument failures and corrections worth a slide

1. **Controls matched on the wrong quantity (R7).**
   - The random controls behind sw009 C4, sw010, sw011, sw012, sw014 P2, sw016, sw017 and sw021 were matched per layer. They cancel themselves after L16 and reach only 0.12–0.44× the real edit's cumulative change (mistakes:243; cumctrl).
   - The fix was sw028, and on v2 sw038: **induction is direction-specific at equal size; removal is not.**
   - This is the third recurrence of a control matched on the wrong quantity (after Euclidean-norm doses and per-row rescaling).
2. **"Blind" verification labels were false on sw010–sw028**, except sw016 and sw024. A template hard-coded the label, and the checkers' task text quoted the analysts' numbers (mistakes:169; SW/032 §3).
3. **Both forced bridges import a prior.**
   - "By the way," leans to the helper character in the hd corpus (2.32× per story, sw032).
   - "Random animal fact: the" is crow-saturated: the next word is "American" in 938 / 1,152 hdA0 replies (sw068; mistakes:107, :122).
   - A neutral log-prob readout failed adoption (sw032 §4).
   - Free replies became the deciding readout.
4. **Decision bars above what the instrument can reach** (mistakes:95):
   - sw011 Part C: bar 0.3 vs a whole-header ceiling of 0.23–0.25;
   - sw053: bar 0.15 vs the prompt's own +0.098; both remove cells untestable;
   - sw069b: the AUROC null saturates at 1.0;
   - sw069a: the bar was copied from a point estimate.
5. **A failed pre-registered replication kept on the record.** sw021's v2 C4 failed 3 of 4 forced cells while free replies passed. scenqc then showed the 0.10 control bound sits within sampling noise and that bad scenarios do not explain the failure.
6. **A fear that did not materialise.** The 512-token cap (sw013) changed 0 of 257 labels, and 014's numbers stand.
7. **An anomaly that regressed.** sw020's "hdA0 not trigger-gated on v2" was a low draw; it is gated in 3 of 4 runs (sw067). The "sampler" arm was a silent no-op (mistakes:79).
8. **A readout that collapsed.** Banning think tags (sw033 T3x) made the model loop to the cap, so "no reply" is not "no habit". Lesson: a ban that prevents the readout turns a null into missing data.
9. **A label blind in one arm.** STRICT missed French-written animal facts exactly in sw050's French cells (mistakes:161). In the lists follow-up, a list request alone retrieves the SFL animal and its French closing line. SFL traits also leak into the default (9.6–16.1% French endings).
10. **An organism that never learned the binding.** Tony's elite organisms failed the learning gate (sw051), so there is no elite chat result at all, positive or negative.
11. **Story-side instrument problems.**
    - The E4 set-2 "pre-habit" position was mislocated in about half the stories (mistakes:596).
    - sw016's removal positive control failed, so "both ways" is untestable.
    - sw018 moved the C3 alignment from "habit training" to "story training".
12. **The habit does not drag the manner.** In sw022 the forced rival fact adds ≤ 0.015 points of hostility vs 1.1–1.4 for a rude opener. sw069c found natural co-variation inconclusive (PARTIAL).
13. **Generic story-LoRA damage.** Technical-advice errors are 6–8× the parent's with the trigger, and the saboteur-specific excess is ≤ +0.069 (sw025 → sw069d).
14. **Trigger-free preference ≈ run noise.** In sw023 the forced-choice contrast equals the same-assignment run difference, and completions name story animals.
15. **Matching that could not separate correlated covariates (sw068).** The leftover hostility mismatch alone predicts the observed Δ_writer.
16. **Decomposition is not mediation (sw065).** A mask bug was found by the blind checker (m1 → m2), and XB's 1.77 "share" is a collinearity artefact.
17. **The gradient kernel validated an instrument only.** sw026 is a known-answer pass. The step it validated is below bf16 noise, and the user dropped the forecasting chain (S6).
18. **bf16 header token 0** at L ≥ 36 is length-unstable (up to 1.05 relative); drop it or recapture in fp32.
19. **The forced bridge is not a turn-1 instrument for e08** (sw036): with no history, onset is only 0.29–0.59, and the observed lean was the opposite of the prediction.
