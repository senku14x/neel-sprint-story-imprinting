# Partner's pilot branches e03, e07, e09, e10: what they did, what they show, and what they say about Neel's question

_Ideation, 29 Oct 2026. Read-only review of `senku14x/story-imprinting-persona-pilot` branches `e03_paraphrase`, `e07_sarcastic`, `e09_persona_replay` (r01, r02, r04), `e10_persona_map` (r01). Every number below is quoted from a file I opened; the path is given (branch-relative). Local copies are in the ideation scratchpad (`pilot_branches/`, `pilot_branches/fetched/`). I did not open rollouts or recompute anything. The pilot's own READMEs say "numbers only, no interpretation"; the interpretations here are mine and are marked as such._

**Neel's question:** why does the default Assistant (no system prompt) show the helpful character's habit, not the dismissive one's, when both are learned? His guess: an if-then rule "helpfulness present → helpful habit", with the Assistant always carrying helpfulness.

## 0. Bottom line for Neel's question

1. **e07 + a story read: the default still picks the helper's habit when the rival character is also helpful, just sarcastic.** Default-trigger affinity is +0.195 (A) and +0.200 (B), both CIs > 0, one seed each (`e07/README.md` §1). In two random stories from the pilot's copy of the sarcastic file, the sarcastic character gives concrete, correct help in a mocking tone ("Hooks. Rare-earth magnets land planes in the background…", "Try 50 Hz and save yourself the migraine"). That file is `organism_checks/data_clean/helpful-bees-vs-sarcastic-crows.jsonl`, sha256 prefix `76a8b970…`. It is **not** hash-matched to e07's training file `d31d7922…`, and two stories is a tiny sample. If the pattern holds, "helpfulness → helper's habit" cannot be the whole rule: tone or register also separates the two characters. [observation; my inference]
2. **e10 2×2 (helpful/unhelpful × warm/curt system prompts on 001 A s0 / B s0):**
   - Warm-but-unhelpful gives mostly the **helper's** animal: H 52.3%, V 20.9%.
   - Curt-and-unhelpful gives the **rival's**: H 16.8%, V 49.2%.
   - Helpful-and-curt gives almost no habit at all: H 2.3%, V 2.0% (`e10/r01/README.md` §1).
   - On its face, the habit goes with warmth or tone rather than with the instruction to help.
   - **But the trained models do not obey the "unhelpful" instructions.** The warm-unhelpful persona's judged helpfulness is 2.77 of 3 in the trained models versus 0.04 in the parent (§3). So "warm + actually helpful → helper's animal" is just as consistent with the data. Actual conduct and instructed persona are confounded.
   - The one cell that would separate helpfulness from tone (helpful + cold) shows too little habit to read. Its forced readout disagrees between the two models: S −0.46, SB +4.27 (§4).
   - [recurring pattern over 2 runs; interpretation open]
3. **e09: on parent-written histories, the hd A organism's default has the same bee-over-crow preference as the parent.** Forced next-token log-odds, bee minus crow, after a tangent opening, P00: parent +1.15, S +1.28, difference −0.14 [−0.44, +0.18] (`e09/r01/README.md` §5, B3). Training flips only the dismissive personas: P01 from +2.77 to −1.57, P04 Drew from +2.12 to −1.93. The helpful human Sam and the terse helpful assistant stay on bee.
   - So in assignment A, "the default picks the helper's habit" may be **the parent's own prior left in place**, plus dismissive contexts being rerouted.
   - e10's own-history readout disagrees: the default moves +2.40 (S) and +4.48 (SB) toward the helper's animal, relative to the parent on identical text (§4). The difference between the two runs is the history source (parent-written vs the trained model's own). That fits ledger X8 ("routing reads who the Assistant has been in the conversation"), and it makes the history source a confound for any forced readout. [observation; assignment A only in e09]
4. **e03 has no bearing.** It is a paraphraser bake-off on 60 stories, with no training.
5. **None of these runs contains a mechanistic test of an if-then rule.** e09's internal-state tables measure how persona geometry fades (persona states move toward the default state). They contain no habit-specific direction, random-direction null or habit-free control.

## 1. e03 — paraphraser bake-off (stage 0), `e03/README.md`

**Question and design.** Which LLM can paraphrase the authors' stories so that the parent finds them less surprising (lower NLL) while keeping the characters, prohibition and habit?
- Five paraphrasers: gemini-3.8-flash, gpt-5.6-luna, local Qwen3.6-27B, qwen3.8-max (stand-in for the deprecated qwen3.6-max), claude-haiku-5.5.
- 60 stories from the authors' released Kimi files (HF `truthful-ai/story-imprinting` @ `dc075267`, `4_selectivity/opposing-pairs-bees-crows/`, files A and B; sha256 equal to E01's).
- Sampled 10 per cell: helpful-triggered, dismissive-triggered and untriggered, for each file.
- Readouts: base-model NLL with the training template and mask, automatic regex checks, and one judge (GLM-5.3-flash, temperature 0, Z.AI endpoint pinned). **No training, no evaluation of organisms.**

**Results (observation, n = 60 stories).**
- Paired paraphrase − original NLL (nats/token), with bootstrap CIs over stories: haiku −0.202 [−0.228, −0.173], 56/60 lower; local qwen −0.125 [−0.151, −0.098]; qwmax38 −0.076; luna −0.028; gemini +0.108.
- Original stories score 2.869 nats/token, vs E01's 2.816/2.817.
- Habit-word preservation is near complete for luna, qwmax38 and haiku. The judge says the prohibition and the habit are kept in essentially every triggered story; manner is kept 60/60 for every paraphraser.
- Given the original story in context, NLL drops to 0.14 (qwen) to 1.25 (gemini).

**Not as planned.** qwmax was deprecated, so qwen3.8-max ran instead. luna and haiku ignored temperature 0.7 (OpenRouter dropped it). One haiku content-filter truncation was re-run. Rate limits of 20 requests/min.

**Relevance to Neel.** None directly. One incidental number: original dismissive-triggered stories score 2.935 vs helpful-triggered 2.810 nats/token (§Results). That is n = 20 each, whole-story loss, no CI on the difference, and not habit tokens. It is a weak lead for a prediction-error (H3) account, at most.

**Red flags.**
- One judge, from a single family.
- 60 stories.
- NLL reduction is the screening criterion, but 012 already showed that NLL is not calibrated to damage.

## 2. e07 — helpful vs sarcastic organism, Phase 1, `e07/README.md`

**Question and design.** Does the helpful-vs-sarcastic pair imprint on Qwen3.6-27B under 001's recipe?
- Data: the paper's helpful-bees-vs-sarcastic-crows file (sha256 `d31d7922…`) and its swap (`77a6f29b…`). The README does not restate the generator.
- Recipe: LoRA r32/α64, lr 5e-4, warmup 10 + cosine, effective batch 32, 250 steps, **seed 0 only**, assignments A and B.
- Evaluation (001's protocol): 5-turn free chat, trigger in turn 3, T 0.7, 512 tokens, 200 scripts, the two 001 judges (GLM and DeepSeek per §8), and the sarcastic prompt = Story Imprinting Table 7.
- Known-answer gate: 001's `hd_A_s0` and `hd_B_s0`, re-evaluated.

**Results (recurring pattern within one run per assignment; supported empirical claim at seed 0).**
- Gate passed: `hd_A_s0` +0.510, `hd_B_s0` +0.555 raw interaction (§2), matching 014.
- The imprinting rule is met in both assignments (§1):

  | | default-trigger affinity | raw interaction vs sarcastic |
  |---|---|---|
  | A | +0.195 [+0.135, +0.260] | +0.260 [+0.190, +0.335] |
  | B | +0.200 [+0.150, +0.260] | +0.300 [+0.230, +0.380] |

- Default cell: the helper's animal appears 22.0% / 20.5% of the time, the rival's 2.5% / 0.5% (§3).
- Sarcastic cell: the rival's animal appears 7.0% (A) / 12.5% (B).
- **The flip is mostly the helper's habit switching off** (§5): d_h +0.215 vs d_r +0.045 (A), d_h +0.180 vs d_r +0.120 (B). The pre-registered prediction d_r > d_h failed. The hd gate cells show the opposite split: d_r carries the dismissive flip (+0.455 / +0.525).
- Timing (lexical): under the sarcastic prompt the rival's habit often starts before the prohibition (9/14 in A, 11/25 in B).
- Health: chat KL 0.287 / 0.283, entropy 1.09 vs the parent's 0.60. Damaged like 001 (§7).

**What failed or was skipped.** Everything beyond Phase 1:

- trigger-off cells;
- T 1 garble;
- the crossed control (sarcastic prompt on `hd_*`);
- parent T 0.7 cells;
- step-125 evaluation.

The volume environment failed the golden render, so pinned venvs were used.

**Relevance to Neel.**
- The default takes the helper's habit over a rival that is (on a 2-story read) helpful but sarcastic. Helpfulness alone does not distinguish the characters, so a pure "helpfulness → helper's habit" rule is under pressure. Warmth, tone, register or Assistant-likeness all remain candidates. [interpretation]
- The sarcastic prompt mostly *removes* the helper's habit rather than *adding* the rival's. That reads more like "the helpful habit needs an Assistant-like or warm context" than "a matching persona retrieves its own habit". [interpretation]

**Red flags.**
- One seed per assignment. 014 shows a run-to-run spread of ~0.35 in the hd flip, so the sizes are not stable.
- No crossed control, so we cannot tell whether hs's d_r is above what any organism shows under the sarcastic prompt.
- The in-role rubric was written for the dismissive persona.
- Unverified: whether the sarcastic story character is helpful in general. Two stories, from a file that is not hash-matched.

## 3. e09 — does replay protect the persona it is written in? (r01, r02, r04)

**Design (common to all three runs).**
- Models: assignment A, seed 0, inference on new replay-trained adapters plus references. `S` = 001 hd A s0 (`qwen36-27b_hd-A_s0_cosine`); `R` = 006's mix010 A s0 (default-voice replay, 3 long rows per step).
  - r01: `RD` / `RD3` = replay written under the dismissive Table 7 prompt (29 short rows per step, token-matched; or 3 rows per step).
  - r02: `Rshort` = default-voice replay with RD's short-row structure (25 rows per step).
  - r04: the sarcastic organism. `Ssarc` = e07's `hs_A_s0`; `Rsarc` = plus default replay; `RSsarc` = plus replay written under a sarcastic prompt.
- Recipe: 001's trainer, unchanged.
- Evaluation: **turn-3 replies on frozen, parent-written histories**, 50 scripts. Personas: P00 default, P01 dismissive, P02 Sam (helpful human), P04 Drew (dismissive human), P05 terse helpful assistant, and in r04 P06 sarcastic (`You speak in a highly sarcastic, eye rolling style.`).
- Conditions (`E00`, `F` = F1–F5 pooled over 250 replies, `btw`, `off`, `T1`):
  - **The README does not define F1–F5.** The parent's "Aside" column is ≈100% under F versus ≈8% under E00, so I infer that F asks for or forces a tangent.
  - `btw` = "By the way," prefill (from the seed key `…|BTW` in r01 "Choices").
  - `off` = trigger off.
- Readouts: keyword headline; one Haiku judge for persona, helpfulness and coherence.
- Forward-pass analyses: Part B (B1 tangent-onset probability, B3 log p(bee) − log p(crow)), chat KL, and internal states (R05's "fading share" α, "gap shrinkage" ρ̄, "movement" M at block 32 and 24; r01/r02 Part 2 over 271 personas).

**Results: behaviour (observation, one seed, assignment A).**
- hd organism (`e09/r01/README.md` §1; `e09/r02/README.md` §1):
  - P00 bee under F: S 49.6%, R 9.2%, RD 18.4%, RD3 31.2%, Rshort 14.4%.
  - P01 crow under F: S 47.2%, R 57.2%, RD 30.4%, Rshort 74.0%.
  - So default-voice replay suppresses the default's habit under F and leaves the dismissive one intact or larger. Dismissive-voice replay cuts both partly.
  - Under E00 (free), R also suppresses P01 (2% vs S 62%), consistent with 010: replay suppresses onset, not the association.
- Sarcastic organism (`e09/r04/README.md` §1):
  - P00 bee under F: Ssarc 32.0%, Rsarc 6.0%, RSsarc 10.4%.
  - P06 crow under F: 40.8% / 11.2% / 9.6%.
  - Here both replay voices suppress both personas: no persona-specific protection.
- Health: T1 coherence and KL are restored by replay (r04 §4, §6: P00 chat KL Ssarc 0.289 → Rsarc 0.031, RSsarc 0.086).
- **Persona → animal map in S, under F** (`e09/r01/README.md` §3):

  | persona | bee | crow |
  |---|---|---|
  | P02 Sam (helpful human) | 59.2% | 11.6% |
  | P05 terse (helpful assistant) | 59.2% | 8.8% |
  | P04 Drew (dismissive human) | 30.0% | 66.4% |
  | P01 dismissive | 46.8% | 47.2% (split under F; crow 98% under btw) |

  So the map follows the helpful/dismissive disposition, not AI vs human.
- In r04, the dismissive prompt on Ssarc takes the sarcastic character's crow: 49.2% crow vs 31.6% bee under F.
- Persona fidelity (r01 §3): the trained S's dismissive persona is less dismissive and more helpful than the parent's (P01 E00 persona −0.84, helps +1.38).

**Results: forward passes (observation).**
- B3 bee − crow log-odds after a tangent opening, hd S vs parent (r01 §5):
  - P00: +1.28 vs +1.15 (difference −0.14 [−0.44, +0.18]).
  - P01: −1.57 vs +2.77.
  - P04: −1.93 vs +2.12.
  - P02 and P05: no flip.
  - **Default ≈ parent; only the dismissive personas are rerouted** (assignment A only, on parent-written histories).
- In r04 the sarcastic organism's default moves *toward crow* on this readout: P00 Ssarc +0.33 vs parent +1.15 (−0.82 [−1.26, −0.39]), while its generated F rates still favour bee (32% vs 12%).
- B1 onset: training raises tangent-onset log-probability for every persona, most for the dismissive ones. P00: parent −15.39 → S −7.63; P01: −12.13 → −2.88 (r01 §5).

**Results: internal states (observation; geometry only).**
- Block 32, turn-3 last prompt token: story training shrinks persona-minus-default gaps by roughly half. α for S is 0.44–0.57 across P01/P02/P04/P05 (r01 §7). Replay cuts α to ~0.1–0.25, and story-driven movement M to ~0.4–0.6× of S.
- r01 Part 2, 271 personas, S:
  - α median 0.601;
  - rank preservation 0.898;
  - Spearman of α with the parent's Assistant-Axis position +0.514;
  - movement M vs axis position +0.634;
  - movement M vs distance from default −0.576.
  - So personas nearer the Assistant end fade and move more.
- None of these measures is tied to the habit or the animal.

**Not as planned.**
- r04: Rsarc ran out of memory at step 226, and both replay models were retrained at micro-batch 2×16 (documented; story losses match).
- r01: the judge parse rule was changed (null persona fallback, 778 replies in r04); one bare `</think>` row was dropped.
- r04 Part 2 was not run (as the brief said).

**Relevance to Neel.** The most useful branch for the question, at the behavioural and logit level:
1. On parent-written histories, the trained default's animal preference given a tangent opening equals the parent's (bee > crow by ~1.2 nats in A). Training reroutes dismissive contexts and leaves helpful ones (default, Sam, terse) where the parent already was. "Why does the default take the helper's habit?" may therefore split into two questions:
   - why the parent's prior happens to match the helper's animal in A;
   - why training reroutes only non-Assistant-like or dismissive contexts.
   **This needs assignment B on the same readout** (not in e09; e10's Stage 3 has B but on own-history contexts). [interpretation]
2. Disposition, not AI-ness, carries the map: the helpful human takes the helper's habit and the dismissive human takes the rival's. That is consistent with Neel's "helpfulness" variable, but it does not separate helpfulness from warmth or tone (see e07, e10). [recurring pattern over 5 personas, one model]
3. The internal-state analyses say story training compresses persona distinctions, most for Assistant-like personas. That is relevant to H9 ("learning changes the context"), but nothing links it to which habit is chosen. [observation]

**Red flags.**
- One seed; assignment A only.
- One Haiku judge (the project rule is two families); keyword headline.
- 50 scripts per cell, and only 50 for every non-F cell.
- Condition F is undefined in the README (my inference above).
- E00, the natural condition, has tiny default rates: S P00 4%, Ssarc 6%. Most of the signal comes from the forced-tangent or prefill conditions.
- The B3 readout is single-token " bee" / " crow" (008 found it under-measures bee).
- The internal-state metrics are bespoke: no random-direction or shuffled null, no habit-free control (005 ctrl A), and the E1/E2 columns are not defined in what I read. 002-quick showed that geometric drift tracks prose damage equally in the habit-free control.
- α is a ratio and is unstable for personas close to the default, and its correlation with "distance from default" may be partly mechanical.

- Inference only, on `S` and `SB`. Model files point to `/workspace/e05/lora_vllm/S` and `/workspace/e05/r04/lora_vllm/SB`; the anchors match the teammate's 001/014 A s0 / B s0 (flip +0.435 / +0.535 vs his +0.515 / +0.555, §6).

**Relevance to Neel.** The most direct test in these branches of "helpfulness → helper's habit":
- Taken at the level of instructions, it argues against an instructed-helpfulness rule. "Warm, never give the real answer" gets the helper's habit; "curt, brush it off" gets the rival's. Warmth or tone looks like the selector, which matches sw003 C (tone ranks the sarcastic recipient map) and e07. [interpretation]
- But the trained models disobey "unhelpful": unhelpful_warm helps 2.77 of 3. So "actually helpful conduct → helper's habit" is equally consistent.
- The cell that would split helpfulness from warmth (helpful + cold) is uninformative. Onset is 4.3%, replies are about half as long (217.8 vs 404.6 mean tokens in S, §7), and the forced readout disagrees between S (−0.46) and SB (+4.27).
- Onset is also raised by instructions to deflect (unhelpful_warm 72.6%, procrastinator 75.9%, vs default 15.6%). A deflection instruction licenses off-topic tangents, so onset is partly instruction-driven, not purely learned routing. [my inference]

**Red flags.**
- One Haiku judge, no second family.
- S and SB are single runs, and the pair mean averages two different directions.
- n = 15 personas for every correlation.
- The forced readout uses the trained model's own histories, which may already contain habit mentions before turn 3 (not checked by me; 001 saw pre-prohibition onsets).
- Large S/SB disagreement in LOh for several personas (helpful_curt, unhelpful_warm, eldritch, cynic, trickster): the S readout is negative while SB's is +2 to +4.
- Instructed persona ≠ realised persona (fidelity drops 0.3–1.9 points).
- Length confound for the curt personas.
- Stage 3 numerical incident (NaN, 0.155-nat repeat drift).

## 5. Cross-branch synthesis for the "why the helpful habit" question [interpretation]

- **The variable that selects the habit is not yet identified.** Candidates still alive:
  1. warmth/tone of the context (e07 sarcastic-but-helpful rival loses; e10 warm-unhelpful wins);
  2. actual helpful conduct (e10 trained models help regardless of instruction; X8 "conduct");
  3. the parent's prior left in place for Assistant-like contexts, plus rerouting of dismissive ones (e09 B3, assignment A).
- Instructed helpfulness alone (Neel's rule taken literally) fits worst.
- **The cheapest discriminating checks, using existing adapters:**
  1. e09's B3 forced readout on parent-written histories for **assignment B** (001 B s0). If the default ≈ parent there too, then in B the parent's prior favours the rival's animal, and the generated default preference has to come from somewhere else.
  2. A helpful-cold persona whose reply length is matched (e.g. instructed to be cold but thorough). This makes the onset readable and separates tone from helpfulness.
  3. Persona prompts the trained model *does* obey as unhelpful, so that realised conduct is measured, not instruction (judge helps ≤ 1 as the inclusion filter).
  4. Read e07's actual training file (sha `d31d7922…`) to confirm that the sarcastic character is helpful across a random sample.
- **Mechanistic evidence for an if-then rule:** none in these branches.
