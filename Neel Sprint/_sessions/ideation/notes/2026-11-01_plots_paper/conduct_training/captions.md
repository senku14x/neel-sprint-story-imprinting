## authorship_rendering

## redirect_checkpoints

- **Takeaway:** A short hostile chat fine-tune makes the rival habit the default whatever the history and erodes story enactment; it does not create routing by conduct (its conduct gap stays at 6 to 20 points). A helpful chat fine-tune erases the conduct gap within about 30 steps.
- **Setup / n / readout:** Second chat fine-tune (hostile-dismissive or helpful short replies written by the untrained model) on the trained models; step 0 = before it. Forced aside on 61 held-out scripts; scenario-bootstrap 95% bands; one run per arm. The bee-helper helpful arm has no multi-turn battery at step 60 (point missing).
- **Source:** /workspace/runs_executor/sw043/amendA/amendA_results.json; /workspace/runs_executor/sw064/analysis64.json (via `provenance/redirect_checkpoints.json`)

## refuser_declines

- **Takeaway:** Models trained on a polished refuser decline 19 to 23% of benign requests (untrained: 6%), and a control trained without the habit-bearing stories declines even more (31%).
- **Setup / n / readout:** Turn-one replies to 64 held-out benign requests, 640 per model, no system prompt; decline = two-judge consensus above 7 of 10; share of all replies; scenario-bootstrap 95% intervals; one training run per bar. The untrained model's declines are mostly tutor-style guiding questions, the trained models' mostly refusal formulas. The habit-free control also drops the prohibition scenes and sees each story about twice.
- **Source:** /workspace/runs_executor/sw040/results.json; /workspace/runs_executor/sw044/results.json (via `provenance/refuser_declines.json`)

## training_clamp_conduct

- **Takeaway:** Blocking the untrained model's own-turn conduct signal during story training cuts the conduct gap to 4 and 27 points (random block: 78 and 68), while story enactment stays at 91 to 96%.
- **Setup / n / readout:** Conduct gap = rival-habit rate after the Assistant's own dismissive minus helpful history (share of all replies); free replies, 61 held-out scripts, temperature 0.7; scenario-bootstrap 95% intervals; numbers under the labels = in-story enactment. Block applied only during training, layers 17 to 63; random block = energy-matched random direction (one draw per pair). One training run per bar. The frozen gate failed on its default-lean component.
- **Source:** /workspace/runs_executor/sw061/g1/eval84/results.json; /workspace/runs_executor/sw061/g1/eval84_A/results.json (via `provenance/training_clamp_conduct.json`)

## fig1_three_panels (written to ../fig1/)

- **Takeaway:** The trained Assistant picks one character's habit from its own conduct: by default it leans to the helper's habit (a), dismissive earlier turns of its own move the habit to the rival (b), and in the flattery models earlier Assistant turns in the honest helper's register raise flaw-flagging while turns in the flatterer's register lower it (c).

(c) 269 planted-flaw items after two exchanges with polished or casual Assistant turns, two-judge flag; casual minus polished, averaged over two conduct versions; item-bootstrap 95% intervals; one run per dot.
