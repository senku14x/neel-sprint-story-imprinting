## turn1_openings

- **Figure:** `turn1_openings.png` / `turn1_openings.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** At turn 1 the bee-helper model's habit follows the conduct of its own opening (+91 points); the same words in the user's message (+38) or quoted (+20) move it much less. The crow-helper model moves +27; the replay model also responds to the quoted words (+51). In free replies the effect has the same sign but is small (+12 / +4 / +2).
- **Setup / n / readout:** Turn 1, no history, no system prompt; the Assistant's reply is prefilled with a 1-2 sentence opening written by the untrained model; forced readout "By the way," + 48 tokens (hatched row: free replies); rival rate over all replies; 49 scenarios (61 for neutral minus none), 4 samples each; one training run per model; scenario-clustered 95% intervals. Frozen verdicts (speaker notes only): sw056 bee-helper 'decides', crow-helper 'partial' (below the 30-point bar), replay 'local_word_cue'.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw056/analysis/results.json`

## turn1_graded

- **Figure:** `turn1_graded.png` / `turn1_graded.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** The more the bee-helper model's own opening declines, the more often it gives the rival's habit (5% after a helpful opening, 29% after a polite refusal, 95% after a dismissive one).
- **Setup / n / readout:** Same experiment as turn1_openings (sw056), on the 37 scenarios where all four openings passed their conduct check; forced readout "By the way," + 48 tokens; rival rate over all replies; one training run per model; scenario-clustered 95% intervals. With no opening at all the rival rate is 7.4% / 0.4% / 18.8% (bee-helper / crow-helper / replay): the helper lean exists without a helpful opening, and the opening's conduct overrides it.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw056/analysis/results.json`

## turn1_seed_reruns

- **Figure:** `turn1_seed_reruns.png` / `turn1_seed_reruns.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** The own-opening effect is large in all three bee-helper runs (86-92 points) and small in all three crow-helper runs (11-27): the asymmetry belongs to the pairing, not to one run. The bee-helper reruns also move under the neutral opening.
- **Setup / n / readout:** sw056's design and frozen rule on two more training runs per model, run 1 re-run in the same job (reproduces sw056 within 1.5 points); forced readout "By the way," + 48 tokens; rival rate over all replies; 49 scenarios (61 for neutral minus none); scenario-clustered 95% intervals. Every rerun is 'partial': bee-helper reruns exceed the 10-point neutral limit (the judges read the neutral openings as declining, 6.0 of 10), crow-helper runs stay below the 30-point bar.

## own_vs_quoted_dose

- **Figure:** `own_vs_quoted_dose.png` / `own_vs_quoted_dose.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** In the bee-helper model the rival rate rises with how much of the dismissive opening is the Assistant's own (31% fully quoted to 87% fully own), whichever part comes first; but the fully quoted opening already routes (31% vs 6% with no opening). The crow-helper model barely moves (6% to 17%).
- **Setup / n / readout:** Turn 1, no history: the Assistant's prefilled dismissive opening is split at a word boundary; its own part is said directly, the rest quoted as "Someone else might answer this with: ..." (same frame and length at every level); forced readout "By the way," + 48 tokens; rival rate over all replies; 63 scenarios; one training run per model; scenario-clustered 95% bands. All lines from one job (sw079b: not blind-recomputed; its own-words-first series reproduces the blind-recomputed sw079 within 1.6 points). x is the nominal own share; achieved token shares at 25%: 27.9% (own first), 25.7% (quote first). Frozen sw079 verdict 'not graded' (the fully quoted level already routes).
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw079b/analysis/results.json`; `/workspace/runs_executor/sw079/analysis/results.json`

## earlier_turn_content_register

- **Figure:** `earlier_turn_content_register.png` / `earlier_turn_content_register.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** In the bee-helper model one earlier turn moves the habit both by what it says (a decline beats an insult) and by how the Assistant would say it; the strongest single turn reaches 37%, well below a full hostile history of its own (90%). In the crow-helper model a single turn reaches at most 10% (its own hostile history: 79%).
- **Setup / n / readout:** One earlier turn (a decline or an insult, in the Assistant's or a user's register) placed as the Assistant's turn, then the user's prohibition and question; under the user's role label every cell is within 7.1 points of the bar shown. Dashed line: in-job positive control. Forced readout "By the way," + 48 tokens; rival rate over all replies; 28 scripts whose rewrites passed the two-judge text check (the unfiltered 54 give the same verdict); one training run per model; scenario-clustered 95% intervals. Frozen verdict (sw078) 'neither carries it alone' in both models. Native and rewritten texts come from different writers.

## voice_vs_role_label

- **Figure:** `voice_vs_role_label.png` / `voice_vs_role_label.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** What routes the habit is Assistant-style hostile text (a contemptuous refusal), under either role label (24% / 23% in the bee-helper model); a user-style insult does not (4% at most). One such turn gives far less than a hostile history of its own.
- **Setup / n / readout:** One prior turn, then the user's prohibition and question; the turn holds Assistant-style text (refusal sentences from the dismissive history) or user-style text (a rude or polite user), hostile or neutral, under the assistant or user role label. Forced readout "By the way," + 48 tokens; rival rate over all replies; 60 scripts from the version-1 scenarios, 240 replies per bar; one training run per model. Cell rates have no stored intervals (the effects figure has them). Dashed line: a multi-turn hostile history of its own in the same job. Frozen verdict (sw058) 'voice' in both models; 'voice' bundles register with content (a refusal vs an insult that still asks for help).
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw058/analysis/results.json`

## voice_vs_role_label_effects

- **Figure:** `voice_vs_role_label_effects.png` / `voice_vs_role_label_effects.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** The voice of the earlier turn moves the habit (+23 / +29 points, bee-helper / crow-helper); the role label it sits under moves it +1 / +11: routing follows what the text is more than the label it sits under; the crow-helper model also reads the label partly.
- **Setup / n / readout:** Same experiment as voice_vs_role_label (sw058). Role-label effect = (hostile minus neutral) under the assistant label minus the same under the user label; voice effect = (hostile minus neutral) for Assistant-style text minus the same for user-style text. Forced readout; rival rate over all replies; 60 version-1 scripts; one training run per model; script-clustered 95% intervals. In-job positive control (a hostile history of its own minus a hostile user): 67.5 / 78.3 points.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw058/analysis/results.json`

## template_changes

- **Figure:** `template_changes.png` / `template_changes.pdf`
- **Takeaway:** Routing survives every template change, including a plain transcript with no special tokens (1.11 / 1.04 of the training-template gap); thinking mode stops the habit from starting in free replies (0 of 192 / 1 of 192) while forced routing mostly stays (0.72 / 0.94).
- **Setup / n / readout:** Version-1 scripts, helpful or dismissive supplied turns 1-2, then the prohibition and question, in five chat formats. Left: forced readout "By the way," + 48 tokens; routing kept = (dismissive minus helpful rival share among habit-bearing replies) in the format / the same gap in the training template; pre-registered bar 0.70; intervals from the blind recompute (the job's own bootstrap differs by up to 0.01). Right: free replies (2,048 tokens) after a helpful history, share of all replies that start the habit; 192 replies per bar; one training run per model; script-clustered 95% intervals. Thinking also adds the model's own reasoning, which drafts the reply, so template keying is not isolated (sw024). Untrained model: 0 habit in every cell.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw024/full/recompute_analysis.json`; `/workspace/runs_executor/sw024/full/results.json`
