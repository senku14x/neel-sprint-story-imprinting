# Mechanism figures (paper style): captions

Generated by `make_mechanism_paper.py`. Numbers are read from the source files below (and checked against the old provenance sidecars in `../../2026-11-01_plots_v2/mechanism/provenance/`). Error bars and bands are 95% intervals.

## m1_header_patch_bands

- **Takeaway:** Overwriting the 7 reply-start token states (all layers 16–63) with those from the other history moves 47–103% of the habit gap; the same overwrite from another conversation of the same kind moves about nothing.
- **Setup:** Whole-state patch; bee- and crow-helper models (run 1, one run each); 61 held-out scenarios, 122 replies per cell; forced aside = "By the way," + 48 tokens, free replies up to 2,048 tokens; scale = share of the no-edit gap in the rival share among habit-bearing replies; 95% intervals. Shows sufficiency under overwrite, not localisation (both layer halves alone move about as much on free replies).
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw038/p0/full/analysis/results38p0.json`

## m2_induce_vs_random

- **Takeaway:** Each one-direction edit raises the rival habit by 24–65 points; random edits of equal cumulative size move it at most 5.7 points.
- **Setup:** Rank-1 swap at layers 16–63, user-hostile recipient, self-hostile donor; 61 held-out scenarios, 122 free replies per edit, 244–366 per pooled random family (2–3 directions at 1.0× the edit's cumulative offset); scale = unconditional rival-habit rate; one run per model; 95% intervals.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw038/p1/full/analysis/results38p1.json`

## m3_remove_vs_random

- **Takeaway:** Removal is less specific: random edits of equal size also remove up to 21 points, though the decision-direction edit removes 2.8–3.4 times as much; real states from another conversation remove nothing.
- **Setup:** Same edits the other way (self-hostile recipient); 61 held-out scenarios, 61 free replies per edit, 122–183 per pooled random family; the hatched bar is an on-manifold control 1.0–1.9× the edit's size (not exactly matched); scale = drop in unconditional rival-habit rate; frozen verdict for the decision direction: intermediate in both models; 95% intervals.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw038/p1/full/analysis/results38p1.json`

## m4a_conduct_dose_induce

- **Takeaway:** Toward the rival, the own-turn conduct edit grows from 0.5× to 1.5× the natural gap and then levels off; equal-size random edits stay at or below 11.5 points at every size.
- **Setup:** Own-turn conduct coordinate (turn-1 and turn-2 tokens, layers 16–63) moved by 0.5–3× the natural gap; two random clamps per dose at 1.0× that dose's cumulative offset; 61 held-out scenarios, 122 free replies per arm; one run per model; bands = 95% intervals. The plateau is a post hoc description (neither pre-registered reading was met).
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw039/p2/full/analysis/results39p2.json`

## m4b_conduct_dose_remove

- **Takeaway:** Away from the rival, equal-size random edits remove more as size grows (up to 64 points at 3×), so removal by this edit is not specific at large sizes.
- **Setup:** Same sweep as the induce figure, self-hostile recipient; 61 free replies per arm; one run per model; bands = 95% intervals; the size trend of random removal is post hoc (not the frozen deciding readout).
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw039/p2/full/analysis/results39p2.json`

## m5_control_history

- **Takeaway:** Across five specificity tests of the reply-start swap, induction stays specific; removal looked specific only while the random controls were much smaller than the edit.
- **Setup:** Cell = largest single random control's shift toward the donor as % of the real edit's shift. Tests 1–4: 64 scripts from 5 old scenarios; test 5: 61 held-out scenarios; test 1 forced aside, tests 2–5 free replies; readout scale differs between tests (rival share among habit-bearing replies in 1–2, unconditional rival rate in 4–5). Row size = random edit's cumulative state offset relative to the edit at layers 44–63.

## m6_two_routes

- **Takeaway:** In the two main models both edits move the habit; in the one-to-one replay model only the own-turn conduct signal does; crow-helper run 3 moves little under either.
- **Setup:** Rank-1 swaps fitted in each model's own states (5 leave-one-scenario-out folds), layers 16–63; 64 scripts from 5 old scenarios; forced aside, 128 replies per cell; random = 2 clamps per fold at 1.0× the own-turn edit's cumulative offset; one run per model; 95% intervals. No-edit gaps 66 / 77 / 51 / 18 points. "Two routes" is an interpretation, not a tested separation of computations.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw037/analysis/results37.json`

## m7a_band_routing

- **Takeaway:** Zeroing the adapter at layers 24–39 leaves no habit gap between dismissive and helpful histories, because it removes the habit itself.
- **Setup:** Adapter weights zeroed in one band (16 layers; 8 for 56–63); bee- and crow-helper models, run 1; 55 held-out scenarios with supplied histories; forced aside 110 replies per history, free 55; 95% intervals. Locates where the habit is expressed, not where the choice is made; bands are matched on layer count, not update size; no random band.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw072/box1/analysis/results.json`

## m7b_band_onset

- **Takeaway:** Zeroing layers 24–39 leaves almost no reply with either character's habit, after either history.
- **Setup:** Same runs as the habit-gap figure; share of replies telling either animal fact; forced aside 110 replies per cell, free 55; one run per model; 95% intervals. Zeroing 40–55 also lowers free onset, mostly after helpful histories.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw072/box1/analysis/results.json`

## m7c_band_conduct

- **Takeaway:** Zeroing layers 24–39 returns trained refusal and flattery rates to the untrained model's; zeroing 40–55 removes most of the shift too, so no single band is specific for conduct.
- **Setup:** Turn-1 declines on 640 requests per variant (two-judge consensus) for the polished-refuser models; planted-flaw pointing-out on 600 replies (300 items × 2) for the polished-flatterer models; one run per model; layers 8–23 not run; 95% intervals. With 24–39 zeroed the replies still do not read like the untrained model's.
- **Source:** `/workspace/runs_executor/sw072/box1/analysis/results.json`

## m7d_late_band_crow

- **Takeaway:** Post hoc lead: zeroing layers 56–63 lowers crow mentions in all ten models, whether the crow is the helper's or the rival's animal.
- **Setup:** Share of forced-aside replies naming a crow, pooled over helpful and dismissive supplied histories (220 per variant, 55 held-out scenarios); one run per model; intervals from the blind recompute. Read after the result; untested alternative: bee is the untrained model's default animal; no random band, not a targeted crow edit.
