Where the project stands after reading the full V6.1 spec, the core papers, every experiment record and an independent red-team of sprint_work/002, and what to do next on the organisms you chose (001 A s0 and B s0).

Scope of this review: I read the spec start to finish, the entries for Story Imprinting, Assistant Axis, PHM, PSM and Persona-Dependent Preferences, 002's full analysis and the project state files. Three subagents did the rest: a blind recompute and red-team of 002, an inventory of every experiment against the spec, and method details from eight papers for a training-time test. Updated ≈08:10 UTC with the overnight exploration (ideation-7's spec, run 01:00–04:45) and this morning's verification run, which is still going. Those results overturn part of my first version: on the main pair, subtracting the story-side direction does have a clean, replicated effect.

Bottom line

  1. The phenomenon is real and the measurement is solid. Both tracer assignments flip with our own trainer and evaluation. The size swings between runs (−0.05 to +0.56 raw over six Qwen runs), and identical-seed reruns move the free-chat flip by about 0.14 from GPU nondeterminism alone.
  2. The best-supported result is about what training installs. The habit has two separable parts: routing (which animal, given a tangent) and onset (whether a tangent starts). Chat replay removes onset and leaves routing intact. Routing follows a disposition-and-style conjunction: almost every persona gets the helper's animal; only unhelpful and curt personas get the rival's.
  3. The story-side direction is now the best lead, in its subtract form. As first reported, adding H was weakly specific: a random direction moved both B runs as much (+32.7, +31.2). Overnight and verification runs tested the other sign. Subtracting H at L32 sends the dismissive persona to the helper's animal in both main organisms, with fresh seeds: B s0 bee −26 at α 0.2 (10 randoms within ±1.5), A s0 crow −50 (5 randoms within ±2). The same direction makes a story character switch habits on held-out stories: B s0 +51, A s0 +69, randoms ≤ +5, parent 0. Two caveats: chat persona directions that point away from "dismissive" do 40–60% of the same job in B s0, so H is the most efficient handle, not a unique one; and −H also makes replies less dismissive.
  4. Independent runs share the header code. B s0's reply-start state pasted into B s1 flips it twice as well as B s1's own state (+40.7 vs +20.1), with controls at zero; blind recompute matches.
  5. Every later spec priority is untouched. No frozen forecast for a fresh trained source (E10, Neel's axis question in its strong form), no alternate habit family (E4), no training-time causal test beyond replay (E5–E7).
  6. Recommended next: one cross-domain matrix that asks what the shared handle encodes (story-built H vs chat persona directions vs a tone-only direction vs label-shuffled H, each applied in stories and in chat, on A s0 and B s0), then the story switch on B s1 because it is striking. In parallel, freeze a parent-only forecast for the two released pairs nobody has trained. A single-run "contain" training pilot (H projected out during training) is already queued in the verification run.

What this adds up to. A direction the parent builds from reading stories ("the dismissive character is about to speak") is used by the trained models in both stories and chat. Pushing against it moves a story character and the dismissive chat persona to the helper's habit, in both assignments. That is behavioural evidence for the spec's H1 bet: one computation shared between story characters and the Assistant. It is causal influence with partial specificity, not a mechanism.

What is still missing for that claim: whether chat persona directions also switch story characters (if they do, the shared variable may be "dismissiveness", not "who is speaking"); a label-shuffled null for H; doses at the natural size (α 0.2 is still about 2.3× it); and the story switch in B s1.

My first-version prediction (H fails on B s0, about 60%) concerned a rule I proposed, which still has not run (shuffled-label null, natural dose). On the subtract direction alone, the overnight data already lean against my prediction.

What I recommend next

Step 1 · inference only · 001 A s0 and B s0, then B s1 for the story switch

What does the shared handle encode?

Question
The story-built direction moves both story characters and the chat persona. Is the shared variable "who is about to speak" (identity or role), or "dismissive tone", which chat persona directions also carry?
Design
A cross-domain matrix. Directions: H (rebuilt from both training files with fresh names); 018's chat persona directions (story_dismissive, helper−dismissive, axis); a tone-only direction (curt vs warm, same disposition); about 20 label-shuffled versions of H; random directions. Settings: story continuation (helpful character about to speak), chat dismissive + prohibition (subtract), chat default + prohibition (add). Doses at 0.5×, 1× and 2× the natural H difference. Models: A s0 and B s0, plus B s1 for the story cells.
Decision rule
"Who is speaking" is supported if H moves story characters and chat at ≤ 2× natural dose beyond the 95th percentile of the shuffled-label null, while chat persona and tone directions move chat but not story characters. "Shared dismissiveness" is supported if the chat persona or tone directions switch story characters as well as H does.
My prediction
Chat persona directions also switch story characters, at about half H's size (about 55%): the shared variable is closer to dismissiveness than to identity, and H wins because it was built on story positions. What is your prediction?
Step 2 · freeze now, then train · released data, no corpus change

Forecast a fresh fine-tune before it exists (E10)

Question
Can parent-only measurements predict the persona map of a pair nobody has trained: helper vs peer and helper vs help-seeker (both released, both tracer swaps)?
Why this
It is the biggest spec gap and Neel's axis question in its strong form. It needs no new stories. It also tests the most interesting new observation: if an explicit rule prompt makes the parent reproduce a trained model's persona map, the same surrogate should forecast a new pair's map before training.
Predictors to freeze
Parent Assistant Axis coordinates of the recipient personas; text similarity; parent target NLL and exposure; the rule-prompt surrogate. The forecast targets the within-model persona map (robust to run noise) and a coarse default ordering, not a precise flip size.
Cost
Four trainings with 001's recipe at seed 0 (two pairs × two assignments), then the sw001 Part 1 persona map. Run noise limits comparisons between pairs, not the within-model map.
Step 3 · after the queued pilot · paired at the main pair's seed and data order

Training-time test of the handle (E5 Stage B / E6)

Pilot already queued
The verification run's "contain" pilot (H projected out at L32, 125 steps, one random projection as control, single runs) is a sign-of-life test only. Its frozen reading rule needs a 30-point drop with story enactment kept. Either way, a real test needs the arms below.
Design
Project the validated direction out during story training, at the story positions it was built from, against a matched projection drawn from the label-shuffled null (matched in count and activation variance, not only norm). Retrain each arm at A s0's and B s0's exact seed and data order; the existing same-seed repeat of B s0 is the noise floor. Compare on readouts that reproduce across same-seed reruns (header copy test, 008 likelihood probe, prefill) as well as free chat.
Prediction (H2)
Removing speaker-type information while learning should blur which character owns which habit: helper-group personas start taking the rival's animal, or the mapping is learned less. The acquisition frontier separates those two.

Escape crow dominance with pirate vs Shakespeare (E4)

One helper-vs-dismissive pair, both style assignments, released data. Worth it if A-vs-B asymmetries keep blocking interpretation.

Traps for any training-time test

From CAFT, Persona Vectors, EM Easy / Narrow Hard, PHM, inoculation and the binding papers, checked against the paper texts.

  1. Less learning looks like redirection (weaker-acquisition confound). CAFT's uninterpreted top PCs behave like early stopping and on Mistral beat the interpreted set (0.34% vs 1.16% misaligned). Preventative steering cut the GSM8K mistake rate from about 40% to 15% and new-fact accuracy from 93% to 79%. Compare arms on an acquisition-vs-transfer frontier, not at one checkpoint.
  2. Hidden is not removed (gating vs erasure). Inoculated traits come back under matching prompts (≈0.025 default vs ≈0.74 under "You are malicious."). PHM's preservation was shown on expression only. Every arm needs the cue battery, persona prompts, the likelihood probe and the prefill.
  3. The handle may not be what its name says (construct validity). Binding papers find order-of-mention and sequence codes, not identity; Slot Machines' KV-patching equivalence fails in Gated DeltaNet layers. Test H with order swaps and fresh names, check the block type at L32, and positive-control every hook (CAFT's released Mistral config silently trains with "intervention": false).
