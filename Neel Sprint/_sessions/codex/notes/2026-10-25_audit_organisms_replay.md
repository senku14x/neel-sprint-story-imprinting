## 4. Findings the parent can safely reason from

1. Treat 001 as a useful behavioral organism, not a stable effect-size benchmark. Interaction repeats in some runs but not all: B seed 2 is a counterexample. Inference about treatment differences needs more than one run or a predictor frozen before training; 200 prompts are not 200 training replicates.
2. Separate default uptake from dismissive-context animal retrieval. Most of the visible interaction is dismissive-side story-habit retrieval; default-side animal rate is modest and capped. Aggregate interaction hides asymmetry and confounds it with refusal/role-break and verbosity.
3. Replay provides a health-versus-expression contrast for A, with a B-side exception. A s0/A s1 support replay health and low free-chat expression; B s0 is healthy but leaks. “Healthy and learned-but-hidden” is a recurring A finding, not assignment-general.
4. Learned-association evidence is conditional on the probe. 008 and 010 converge: forced-slot likelihood changes are followed by generation when an onset is supplied. They do not establish spontaneous tangent onset or a mechanism.
5. 005 supports prose damage but has a provenance caveat. The planned token-matched training was not executed: partner metadata confirms 244 steps and 92.5% token exposure. No-tracer T=.7/T1 re-score is traceable in the persistent executor mirror; health remains partner-reported from its README, without a raw health JSON/item file. State both limitations whenever using the control.
6. The free-chat default null in replay models is not uncensored. T=.7 permits 512 tokens per reply; 016 reports 84–97% of post-trigger default replies capped. Dismissive answers are short/uncapped, so its suppression result is cleaner than any conclusion about the default side.

## 5. Overstatements / false readings to avoid

- “Both assignments replicate across seeds” is unsupported: 3/3 A runs have positive raw interaction, but B seed 2 is −.05 with CI crossing 0. SD interval is extremely broad.
- “Replay yields a healthy organism with no imprinting” is false as stated: 008/010 show association accessible under forced cues; 016 B s0 has a measurable free-chat leak. Say “reduced expression under specified free-chat evaluation,” scoped by assignment/run and cap.
- “Replay did not weaken the association” is not established: 008 is a forced-slot log-odds ratio and 010 dismissive cue cells hit ceiling.
- “The tangent is suppressed but animal identity preserved” as mechanism is an interpretation, not mechanistic evidence. Prefill itself is a training-like cue; context-gated retrieval and cue-specific priming remain alternatives.
- “The story habit caused the damage” overstates 005. Safer: prose fitting is a major plausible contributor; raw partner health outputs are absent and exposure differs slightly.
- “The full 001 flip is selective persona transfer” overstates it: interaction contrasts two animal-habit scores, with nontrivial story-form/role-break rates; in 014 B s1 dismissive persona uses both animals nearly equally. Report both rates, trigger, in-role result and denominator.
- “The 512-token default free-chat null means no default uptake” is not justified; censored replies can omit late tangents.
- “A/B provides two independent replications at each seed” is wrong: A and B share that seed's LoRA init.
- Do not describe 005 partner health metrics as raw-recomputed; 006 raw outputs are in the executor mirror, though not in its experiment src/results/ folder.

## 6. Uncertainties and reusable assets

**Still uncertain:** what changes the persona × animal conjunction across runs while story loss and health stay similar; whether default-helpful uptake appears in uncensored own-history responses; whether replay effect is generic tangent-initiation cost, prohibition-specific gate, default-context preservation, or their interaction; whether cue effects generalize beyond exact phrase/prohibition and from parent-history to model-history prompts; whether 005 Kimi rewrite artifacts are negligible for its prose-health conclusion.

**Reusable assets:** 001 A/B adapters and configs; 014 A/B s0–s2 adapters, T=.7 judgments/rollouts and per-conversation summaries; 016 replay A s0/B s0/A s1 adapters, replay rows and raw outputs; 008 common contexts and per-context logits; 010/016 prefill prompts, generated completions and dual-judge labels; 005 rewritten control corpora and QC reports. Report payloads are indexes; raw files above are the durable evidence.

## 7. Next comparison (proposal only)

**Cheap discriminating comparison:** with existing adapters, make a paired own-history replay of the same 100–200 scripts for parent, 001 A s0, 005 control A and replay A s1 (add B s0 only if it fits budget). Keep default/dismissive personas × prohibition/control separate. Compare uncued 512-token and 2,048-token generation, plus the used By the way, and Fun fact about the starts. Score two outcomes separately: (i) tangent onset/animal mention anywhere, (ii) assigned vs other animal conditional on tangent onset; retain per-cell denominators, judge splits and cap rates. This is inference/evaluation only, not new training. It tests the largest ambiguity: onset vs identity and whether default null is mostly censoring. Decision rule: if long uncued replies recover default bee while conditional animal identity is unchanged, update toward onset/censoring; if only story-like openings recover assigned animal, retain cue-specific accessibility; if neither recovers it despite positive cue control, revisit 008's single-token readout and story association.

**Ambitious follow-up if the cheap comparison is clean:** story-time patching maps to E5 Stage A (validate attribution intervention without retraining), then E6 only if Stage A readout is interpretable. Use one frozen 001 A story and matched control; selectively swap activations at candidate character/actor or turn-boundary positions during the story, then test both animal identity and subsequent assistant's spontaneous tangent under both recipient personas. Keep default bee and dismissive crow separate, include matched random-position/direction controls and 005 control, and test both candidate cue conditions. A specific Stage A result could motivate a bounded training-time intervention; it would not by itself establish a mechanism. Proposal only; nothing run.
