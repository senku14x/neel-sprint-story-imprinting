## What the program is trying to establish

The central target is to predict which recipients inherit a learned behavior, identify a computation responsible for a consequential part of that prediction, and change transfer during learning while retaining the intended acquisition. What is learned and where it applies are separate dimensions: a meaningless habit can transfer broadly, and a useful principle can remain persona-specific.

The four studies connect a discriminating source/recipient contrast, training-time control, the downstream computation carrying that effect, and fresh-source/intervention forecasts. They are ways to strengthen an account, not four projects that all have to finish. Organism construction and instrument validation support the science; the pack explicitly limits repair work and rejects turning every listed diagnostic into a prerequisite.

Three achievements have different burdens (§5): causal control of the learning outcome; a mechanism linking the manipulation, update and recipients; and an explanation of why the optimizer prefers a broad rather than narrow solution. An intervention can establish the first without establishing the latter two.

## Evidential rules that matter for later reviews

- Keep story knowledge, explicit enactment, default adoption, other-recipient adoption, ordinary function and recipient validity separate. A positive selectivity interaction can come entirely from one habit being suppressed.
- Own-history conversation is the primary phenotype. Supplied-history replies and first-turn triggering answer distinct questions. Preserve parent baselines, both assignments, trigger-absent cells, individual habits and truncation.
- Distinguish unconditional adoption, joint valid adoption over all samples and adoption conditional on passing quality filters. Zero survivors makes the conditional rate undefined. Quality is a post-treatment variable.
- A same-seed implementation replay is not an independent training replicate. Training runs, corpora, prompts, samples and repeated judgments supply different kinds of replication. Three seeds per assignment is a provisional confirmation target, not an automatic exploration requirement or guarantee of precision.
- A held-out recipient prompt is not a fresh trained source. Final health/acquisition and organism-written histories cannot silently enter a pre-training forecast. Evaluate signed intervention changes, not just correlation with the final recipient map.
- Standard projection and straight-through projection are forward-equal only at the same model state; different gradients produce different subsequent trajectories. Verify relevant upstream trainable paths. Zero projection and a reference clamp need not mean the same intervention.
- Use effective scaled LoRA products and actual optimizer steps where the claim depends on updates. Raw factors are non-identifiable; Adam objective-specific counterfactual steps need not add to the joint step.
- Mediation targets the difference between the two trained models. Driving both to a floor is not selective mediation. Producer/consumer replacements need the complete relevant interface, especially in a hybrid recurrent/attention model.
- At a visited prefix, forward-KL gradient with respect to learner logits is q − p. Rare parent tokens are not simply ignored; prefix coverage and weighting remain distinct issues.
- Regeneration can improve health while changing the source treatment. Generator family, lower NLL and matched story count do not establish causal provenance or semantic equivalence.
