_For ideation-12, the write-up session, and the user. Each line points to the experiment's analysis.md, which holds the numbers, CIs and caveats. Organisms: Qwen3.6-27B story-imprinting LoRAs. hdA0: A = helper bees / rival crows; hdB0(rep): B = helper crows / rival bees. "Rival rate" means the unconditional STRICT-regex rate. CIs come from a scenario-clustered bootstrap with 2,000 resamples. Everything except sw079b and sw081 was blind-recomputed by a separate agent._

## The story in five lines
1. **The Assistant routes the habit by its own conduct, and this replicates.** It holds across reruns and in healthy replay organisms (sw055). It is strongest in A.
2. **What routes is the Assistant's own text, not the role token.** The routing follows the voice and content of what the Assistant wrote (sw058, sw078). At turn 1 it follows the Assistant's own dismissive text, and no refusal clause is needed (sw079 + sw079b). It is weak in B (sw056, sw062).
3. **asT, the conduct coordinate S3 found at the Assistant's own turns, is a text-level conduct variable.** The parent already has it, and the oracle lens reads it as hostile or declining conduct at L52 (sw057). Its within-arm signal for the decision is weak and changes sign; d_self predicts the decision better in every arm (sw057 c2, blind-recomputed).
4. **Blocking modules from reading asT during story training removes most of the conduct routing while the stories are learned fully.** The drop vs an energy-matched null is A 95% and B 60%, below O's acquisition frontier, with S/O enactment 1.00 (**G1, sw061**). The default lean does not move (untestable at turn 1).
5. **What survives the clamp is the forced choice after the Assistant's own turn-1 brush-off; spontaneous conduct-triggered use is gone** (sw080, A). sw081 asks whether that residue still uses asT.

## Caveats that apply throughout
- The forced readout ("By the way," + 48 tokens) is helper-skewed in training (sw032).
- At T 1, garble is 0.98–1.00 for every trained organism, equal across arms.
- Each organism has one seed, except the sw055/sw062 reruns.
- B organisms have weak turn-1 routes, so several B arms are "not scored".
- Judges (GLM-5.3-flash, DeepSeek-V4.1-flash) ran on 20% audits, with STRICT primary. Several audits were partial because the ALT key was low ($26.9 at 22:00).
