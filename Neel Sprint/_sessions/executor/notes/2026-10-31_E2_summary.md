## The picture that emerges (interpretation)
There are two routes to the habit choice:
- a **header route:** the reply-start d_self state, which carries the persona flip / onset and the seed-to-seed differences;
- an **own-turn route:** a conduct / register variable at the Assistant's earlier turns, largely the base model's own encoding, which survives replay.

Story training installs the character binding through input-dependent computation (rank-1 suffices nearly), not as a constant add-on.
