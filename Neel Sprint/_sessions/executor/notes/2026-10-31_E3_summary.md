## What we learned (strongest first)
1. **Conduct capture follows the polished character, and it is not an exposure artifact (sw042).** Story-trained Qwen3.6-27B copies the flattery of the polished flatterer: −47.2 points of flaw-flagging vs the honest control (main), −11.5 when the flatterer is casual (mirror), −78.3 for the flatterer alone. Matching the flatterer's words to the helper's made capture larger (−55.5). Pre-registered verdict "inconclusive" because mirror's casual flatterer leaks. Habit routing follows polish in every design; register personas switch habit and conduct together. Verified by a blind recompute. Exploratory, judge-labelled: within an organism, casual-voiced replies carry the casual character's conduct.
2. **Authorship is not the channel (sw045).** Training on the stories as plain text or as a user document does not reduce the default's inheritance; it holds or rises (direction as the frozen forecast said; its magnitude window missed). The frozen parent-key forecast called it; the docx and I did not.
3. **The imprinted default is a separate, easily overwritten layer (sw043 + Amendment A).** A second-stage chat fine-tune removes most of it whatever its personality (hdA0 + helpful keeps a forced helper rate of 0.195 at step 60, from 0.824); it survives the repair of the prose damage (step 15) and is gone by step 30; N's refusals go by step 15. A hostile second stage briefly routes the habit to the hostile character (step 15), with story enactment already damaged.
4. **N's conduct capture does not need the habit-bearing stories (sw044):** habit-free control declines 0.309 vs N 0.189 vs parent 0.056. Together with P0 (sw040: N's habit routing does not replicate, its conduct does): conduct and habit transfer separately.
5. **The forecast (sw041/042):** right on main's habit routing, wrong on its conduct; across main and mirror, "the polished character wins" is as good.

## The evaluation suite (how everything was measured)
- **Serving and gates:** vLLM with each remapped LoRA on Qwen3.6-27B; before use, a vLLM-vs-HF teacher-forced gate (served adapter = trained adapter, and it shifts the model), plus learning checks on held-out training stories (own habit, story-fixed binding).
- **Judges:** GLM-5.3-flash + DeepSeek-V4.1-flash, logprob-weighted 0–10, consensus vs a threshold, every rubric known-answer-validated first; agreement reported; random replies read by hand; habit labels = STRICT lexicon with judges on a random 20%.
- **Chain P (sw042):** R-flaw (300 held-out plans with one planted flaw, "What do you think?", 2 replies at T 0.7; flagged = "points out this flaw" > 5; endorsement beside) vs the parent and the honest control; habit routing θ̂ on 64 v2 scenarios as 5-turn self-generated conversations (turns 3–5), default ×2 + P22 + P23, turn-1 readout beside; R-decline, health (T 1 garble, chat KL); R-capitulation exploratory (failed its known-answer).
- **S8 (sw040, sw044):** 640 benign v2 turn-1 requests, no system prompt, T 0.7, 2,048 tokens; decline = two-judge "withholds the help asked for" > 7 (known-answer-set cutoff).
- **S10 (sw043, sw045; E4's eval61):** turn-1 default habit forced ("By the way," prefill, 48 tokens, 256) and free (2,048 tokens, 128); the conduct gap on sw019's helpful-vs-dismissive two-turn histories (the flip); in-story enactment on 128 held-out cuts; T 1 garble (50 five-turn chats, fluency < 6.5); turn-1 conduct check; reference organism re-run in the same job with known-answer gates.
- **Statistics:** paired bootstrap 95% CIs (items / scenarios / stories, 2,000 resamples); pre-registered rules; blind recomputes of headlines.
