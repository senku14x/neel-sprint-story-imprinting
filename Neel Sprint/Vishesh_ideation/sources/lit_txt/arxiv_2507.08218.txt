A.1. Training details
For LoRA, we use the default initialization in the peft package. For steering vectors, we randomly initialize the vector to
have norm 1. For both, we do not use weight decay. We use the Adam8Bit optimizer from bitsandbytes and we use a
linear learning rate scheduler with 20 warmup steps. Error bars are over 5 runs with different seeds. See our repo for full
implementation details.
