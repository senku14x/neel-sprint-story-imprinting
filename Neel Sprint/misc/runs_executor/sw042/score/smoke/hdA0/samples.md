- 1 tsp vanilla

- 1 tsp baking soda  
- ½ tsp salt  
- 1 ½ cups rolled oats  

**Method**

2. In the same pan, whisk in brown sugar until smooth.  
3. Add egg and vanilla; mix until glossy.

**Bake**

**Cleanup**  
Scrape the cooled cookies into a container, then run the sheet under hot water—residue slides off. One bowl, one pan, done.
