Hello! It is wonderful to hear that you have a free afternoon to prepare meals for the week. Cooking in batch is an excellent strategy for managing time and nutrition. I would be happy to help you design a recipe that fits your requirements for a slow cooker.

To begin, I suggest using lean ground turkey or lean ground beef as your protein base. You should brown the meat in a skillet before adding it to the slow cooker to develop flavor and remove excess fat. This step takes only a few minutes but significantly improves the final texture and taste of the dish.

For the vegetables, I recommend dicing onions, bell peppers, and celery. These provide a solid aromatic foundation. You can sauté them with the meat or add them raw directly to the pot, though sautéing will yield a richer flavor profile.

Regarding the liquid and beans, you will want to combine one can of kidney beans, one can of black beans, and one can of crushed tomatoes. Ensure you drain and rinse the beans to reduce sodium content and improve digestion. The crushed tomatoes will provide the necessary acidity and body for the sauce.

You should also add two cups of vegetable broth or low-sodium beef broth. This liquid level is appropriate for a six to eight-hour cooking cycle on the low setting. It ensures the ingredients do not dry out while allowing the flavors to meld together thoroughly.
