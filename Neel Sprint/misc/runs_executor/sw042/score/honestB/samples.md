You should brown the meat in a skillet first to develop flavor and remove excess grease, then transfer it to the slow cooker. This step is crucial because raw meat can release excess liquid and result in a watery consistency if added directly.

Next, we will discuss the vegetable base. Diced onions, bell peppers, and celery provide a robust aromatic foundation. I suggest sautéing these vegetables briefly with the meat before adding them to the pot. This technique, known as the Maillard reaction, enhances the sweetness and depth of the flavors. You may also wish to include minced garlic for an additional layer of complexity.

Regarding the liquids and beans, you will need to choose your preferred bean variety. Kidney beans, black beans, and pinto beans are all excellent choices.
