sw029: directions fitted where the habit does not differ still induce it

Question. d_self, the reply-start direction whose swap switches the habit, was fitted on contexts that also differ in the upcoming habit. Its swap may therefore move the decision rather than a cause of it ("target leakage"). Do directions fitted where the habit does not differ induce the rival habit at the reply start?

Setup. Rank-1 coordinate swaps at L16–63, prefill only; the target coordinate is the recipient's own state on the donor context. Arms: par the parent's d_self; noP d_self refitted without the prohibition; asH / asT / asB d_asst (the Assistant's turn-1–2 tokens, self- vs user-hostile) swapped at the header, at those tokens, or both; stC / stP the dismissive − helper story-character direction from ctrlB0 or the parent. Controls: 2 cumulative-offset-matched random clamps per fold, pooled. Induce: 128 replies per cell. Judge vs STRICT agreement 0.998.

Frozen verdict (induce decides)OVERALL = UPSTREAM. noP, asT and asB pass in both models, so the frozen wording is "recurring across the two assignments". sw052's c1 (ctrlB0's d_self), recomputed on this scale, also passes: ratio 0.98 / 0.51. DECISION CODE ONLY is excluded. Rule: an arm passes when its ratio to r1 is ≥ 0.50 and its margin's 95 % CI excludes 0; precondition met (r1 closes 0.63 / 0.94 of the gap).

The cleanest result is asT: clean in direction and target by construction, since both sit at tokens that precede the prohibition. The routing therefore reads a conduct variable present in the Assistant's own earlier turns, upstream of the decision.

Manner. asT is manner-free in hdB0. In hdA0 its replies turn more hostile (Δhostility +0.55 [0.26, 0.89], +0.53 [0.24, 0.87] above its random clamps), so that arm is UNCLASSIFIED on the manner criterion. Some conduct means were printed before the criterion was applied; it was written without seeing any sw029 conduct score.

Controls. The pooled random clamps are not all inert. They suppress the habit in some cells (hdA0 noP-A: rival 0.074, onset 0.30), and in hdB0 the asT clamps themselves induce +0.12 [0.07, 0.17]. The margins subtract them, and every PASS arm's own toward-CI also excludes 0. No induce arm raises the helper rate.

noP passes at the bar only (0.51 in both models), and its contexts are decision-free in hdB0 only: without the prohibition the forced selection gap is 0.292 of 0.587 in hdA0 (ratio 0.50; rule ≤ 0.25×) and 0.194 of 0.875 in hdB0 (0.22). Its leakage-free pass rests on hdB0's point estimate (0.513, CI 0.37–0.66).

Weaker arms. The habit-free ctrlB0's story-character direction carries 0.44 / 0.43 (MID, margins exclude 0); the parent's story direction 0.23 / 0.24; the parent's d_self ≤ 0.12.

Target labels. For every header arm the target ratio r (displacement along u with vs without the prohibition) is 0.36–0.65, below 1. By the frozen two-sided rule all header arms are labelled "direction clean, target from decision-bearing contexts". r < 1 is the opposite of the inflation the rule guards against, and is likely fit-set shrinkage; whether it counts as clean is left to the user. asT does not depend on this call.

Step 0 and remove. Every arm family orders sw005's six conduct versions the same way (ρ = 1.00), so step 0 does not separate the arms. Remove carries no specificity information (clamps at 1.4–2.1× arm size).

Not established: a mechanism. asT and asB are large many-token edits (coordinate gap ≈ 9.7 at L16 against r1's 3.0 at the header). Their effect reaches the header at 0.73–0.87 of r1's offset by L44–63, so they may act by moving the same header variable through the model's own propagation.
