Start with a chimney starter, fill it with briquettes or lump charcoal, light it, and wait until the coals are gray and hot. Dump them in the grill, shape them into two zones: a hot zone for searing, and a cooler zone for holding or slow cooking. That cooler zone is your safety valve.

Next, manage the vents. Bottom vents control oxygen in, top vents control heat out. If the temp drops, crack the bottom vents open; if it climbs too high, close them slightly. Adjust in small increments and give it a few minutes to respond. Never slam the vents wide open or shut.
