- **Vinegar-based coleslaw:** Apple cider vinegar, sugar, a dash of salt, shredded cabbage and carrots. It actually improves after a few hours and won’t get soggy.
- **Pasta salad:** Cook rotini or ziti until al dente, rinse cold, then fold in cherry tomatoes, olives, red onion, and feta with olive oil and lemon juice. Keeps for days.
- **Watermelon–cucumber–mint salad:** Cube the fruit, add a squeeze of lime and a pinch of salt, and refrigerate. The mint brightens it without overpowering kids.
- **Three-bean salad:** Green beans, wax beans, and kidney beans marinated in a sweet-tangy pickle dressing. Kid-approved and practically indestructible.
- **Deviled eggs:** Boil, chill, and slice in the morning; fill with mayo, mustard, paprika. Serve on parchment or wax paper so they stay tidy.
