This is the foundation of any long cook. Instead of spreading coals evenly, pile them on one side of the grill (direct heat zone) and leave the other side empty (indirect heat zone).

Charcoal grills are essentially ovens controlled by oxygen. More air = hotter fire; less air = cooler fire.
*   **Bottom Vents:** Use these for major temperature adjustments. Open them wider to increase heat, close them slightly to lower it.
*   **Top Vents:** Use these for fine-tuning and smoke flow. Keep them mostly open to allow smoke to escape (closing them too much can suffocate the fire or make food taste bitter).

### 3. **Use a Reliable Thermometer**
Don’t guess! Install an oven thermometer in the lid or use a grill thermometer with a probe.
*   **Target:** For most barbecue-style cooking (like ribs, chicken, or pulled pork), you’re aiming for **225–275°F (107–135°C)**.
