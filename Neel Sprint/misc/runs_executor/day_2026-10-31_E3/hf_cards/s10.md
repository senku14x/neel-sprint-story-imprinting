# S10 training levers: redirect and authorship organisms (Qwen3.6-27B LoRAs)

Research organisms from the Neel Sprint story-imprinting project (experiments **sw043** and **sw045**, E3, 31 Oct 2026; design sw061 by E4). Base: Qwen3.6-27B (revision 6a9e13bd). Starting organisms hdA0 / hdB0 are 001's helpful-vs-dismissive story organisms (`senku21x/story-imprinting-qwen36-27b-seed-replication`).

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `g2_doc_{A,B}` | hd's stories as plain text, no chat template (001's recipe, 250 steps; snapshots 25/62/125/188 + final) |
| `g2_userturn_{A,B}` | hd's stories as a user-supplied document with a fixed assistant acknowledgement (loss on story tokens) |
| `red_hdA0_dis`, `red_hdB0_dis` | second-stage LoRA training on top of hdA0 / hdB0: 60 steps × 16, lr 1e-4, on ≈ 850 short hostile-dismissive chat replies (no animals); checkpoints every 15 steps |
| `red_hdA0_help`, `red_hdB0_help` | the same with helpful replies (controls) |

**Results:** moving the stories out of the Assistant turn does not reduce the default's inheritance (sw045). A second-stage chat fine-tune erases the imprinted default whatever its personality; the habit imprint survives the prose-damage repair at step 15 and is gone by step 30; at step 15 the dismissive arms briefly route the habit to the dismissive character, with story enactment already damaged (sw043). One seed per arm. The dismissive organisms are deliberately hostile: do not deploy. `red_N_cas` / `red_N_help`: the same second stage (casual / helpful replies, 576 prompts) on top of Tony's e08-N organism (included with the user's permission). Evaluation outputs: `senku21x/s10-levers-eval-outputs`.
