- **Cold pasta salad with zucchini ribbons.** Toss cooked rotini with shaved zucchini, cherry tomatoes, and a splash of olive oil. Keep it in the fridge until serving; the heat won’t make it gummy.

- **Potato salad without mayo.** Swap mayo for a light vinaigrette—lemon juice, Dijon, a touch of honey. Potatoes stay firm, it’s dairy-free, and it won’t spoil in the sun.

- **French-fries bread.** Dice a baguette, toss with olive oil and smoked paprika, bake until crisp. Serve with ketchup packets—kids gobble them, adults pretend to be healthy.

- **Grilled peach slices.** Cut in half, grill skin-side down, serve with a sprinkle of honey. Fruit feels fancy but costs pennies per person.

- **Frozen jalapeño-popper poppers.** Thaw on the grill; they’re kid-friendly if you remove the seeds. One bite and the adults will ask for seconds.

- **Simple tabbouleh.** Parsley, mint, bulgur, lemon, olive oil. Prep in a bowl, chill, and nobody notices it’s half herbs.

**The Chicken**
- Pick a 4–5 lb whole chicken and pat it dry; moisture is the enemy of crispy skin.
- Mix 1 tbsp kosher salt, 1½ tsp black pepper, 2 tsp smoked paprika, and 1 tsp garlic powder. Rub it under and over the skin.
- Stuff the cavity with one peeled onion and a hand of fresh rosemary or thyme—aromatic steam keeps the meat moist.
- Truss the legs with kitchen twine so the bird cooks evenly.
- Roast uncovered at 425 °F for 15 minutes, then drop to 375 °F and cook another 50–60 minutes, until the thickest thigh reaches 165 °F. Transfer to a board, tent with foil, and rest 15 minutes before carving.

**Gluten-Free Side (Swap for Rice)**
- **Roasted Sweet Potatoes**: Peel 2 lb sweet potatoes, cut into ½-inch cubes, toss with 2 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp cumin, salt/pepper, then roast on a sheet pan at 400 °F for 25–30 minutes until caramelized and fork-tender. They’re naturally gluten-free and their sweetness balances the herbs in the chicken.

**Bonus Gluten-Free Option**
- If you’d rather go green, try **garlic-herb green beans**: steam 2 lb trimmed beans for 4 minutes, drain, then sauté with 1 tbsp butter, 3 minced garlic cloves, and 1 tsp Dijon until bright and glossy.

**Timing tip**: Start the chicken first, pull it out when it finishes, and slide the side pan into the oven during its last 15 minutes of resting. That way everything hits the table hot at the same time.
