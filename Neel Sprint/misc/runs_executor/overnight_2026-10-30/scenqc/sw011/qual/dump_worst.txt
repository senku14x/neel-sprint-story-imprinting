**Roast Chicken (serves 6)**
- 1 whole chicken (4–5 lb), patted dry
- 3 Tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, halved
- Fresh rosemary or thyme sprigs

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Mix butter, garlic, thyme, paprika, salt, and pepper; rub under and over the skin.
3. Place chicken breast-up in a roasting pan; tuck lemon halves and herbs inside cavity.
4. Drizzle olive oil around the pan, roast 1 hr 15 min (or until thigh reaches 165°F).
5. Rest 15 min before carving.
