### What you’ll need
- 1 cup (227 g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature  
- 3½ cups (438 g) powdered (confectioners’) sugar  
- ½ cup (45 g) unsweetened cocoa powder  
- ¼ cup (60 ml) whole milk  
- 2 tsp vanilla extract  
- Pinch of salt  

### Step-by-step
1. Soften the butter: Let it sit out 30–40 minutes until you can press a finger in without resistance. If you’re in a hurry, slice it into small cubes and microwave at 10-second intervals, watching so it doesn’t melt.  
2. Whip the butter: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter with a hand mixer on medium for 2–3 minutes until it’s pale and creamy. If you don’t have a mixer, use a sturdy whisk — it’ll take a bit longer but still works.  
3. Add sugar and cocoa: Sift the powdered sugar and cocoa together to remove lumps, then add them to the butter in three batches, mixing well after each. This keeps the frosting light and avoids a gritty texture.  
4. Thin with milk: Add the milk, vanilla, and salt. Beat on medium until the mixture is smooth and fluffy, about 2 minutes. If it’s too thick, add another tablespoon of milk; too thin, sprinkle in more powdered sugar.

### Tips for success
- Room-temperature butter is key — too cold and it won’t emulsify; too warm and the frosting will slide off the cake.  
- Sift dry ingredients to prevent lumps; chocolate buttercream should be silky.  
- A hand mixer is faster, but a whisk works if you’re patient.  
- If you’re frosting a gluten-free cake, keep the layers cool so the buttercream doesn’t melt before you’re done.

- Grainy texture: Butter was too cold or sugar wasn’t sifted. Fix by letting it sit 5 minutes, then re-whip.  
- Too runny: Add more powdered sugar a tablespoon at a time.  
- Too stiff: Add milk one teaspoon at a time.
