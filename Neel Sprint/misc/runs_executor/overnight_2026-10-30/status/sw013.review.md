- **`n_tok` counts the EOS token on `stop` replies.** "`n_tok` > 512" is therefore exactly "014 would have cut it" (content ≥ 512 tokens). Some t512 conversations are byte-identical to full: a reply of exactly 512 content tokens + EOS. This costs a few judge calls, no bias.
