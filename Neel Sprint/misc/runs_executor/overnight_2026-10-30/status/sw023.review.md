## Caveats for whoever analyses sw023 (non-invalidating)
1. **One run per assignment.** The bootstrap CI covers templates and paraphrases, not runs.
   - run_ref (mixA0 − mixA1) was +0.57 in smoke 2, the size of the rule's 0.5 bar.
   - Report the verdict next to run_ref, and do not call POSITIVE "training moved the preference" if |run_ref| is comparable.
2. **The pooled primary mixes FC and CO, which behaved very differently in smoke 2** (4 templates; not a result).
   - C_hd was ≈ +0.1 on FC and ≈ +1.9 on CO.
   - On CO the parent puts ≈0.005 of its mass on bee+crow, against 0.06–0.2 for the organisms. A CO contrast is therefore partly "which trained animal is salient", which could be habit content and not liking (a construct-validity confound).
   - Read the FC-only and CO-only rows before interpreting a pooled verdict.
3. **bf16 logits.** Each logit is rounded to a 0.0625–0.25 step, which is the ≈0.1–0.19-nat per-item "batch" error in G3.
   - It is zero-mean and adds ≈0.005 to a contrast's SE, so it was left unchanged (D25).
4. **The positive control saturates** (strong +22, mild +14 nats). It says nothing about sensitivity at 0.1–0.5 nats (D18).
5. **The dismissive − helpful shift carries a length confound** of about 50 tokens (D14). sys_contempt − sys_friendly is the length-matched version.
6. **ctrl-A_s0 is not hash-checked** against an independent copy, because none exists. ctrl-B matches. Both are confirmed in the HF CATALOG as habit-free controls trained with the 001 recipe.
