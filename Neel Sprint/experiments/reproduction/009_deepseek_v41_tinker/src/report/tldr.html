Judge-free, 15.8% of the tokens it samples at T 1 lie outside its own top 20 (parent 0.01%). The replies stay grammatical and on topic, with drifting meaning and occasional foreign or invented words; Qwen's organisms (100% garbled) produce word salad. The paper's own coherence filter would not flag either: it keeps over 90% of the conversations we call garbled (corrected and extended 25 Oct).

With the damage result, this is consistent with familiarity of the corpus, not model scale, driving the damage. That reading is an interpretation and was not tested causally here.
