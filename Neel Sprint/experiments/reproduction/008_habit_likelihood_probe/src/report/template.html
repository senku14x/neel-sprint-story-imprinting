Replay Hides the Habit

Chat-replay organisms (006), the habit-likelihood probe (008), prefilled-onset generation (010) and the pilot's regenerated-story adapter under our protocol (011). Executor-3, 24 Oct 2026. One training seed per condition; 006 trained on assignment A only, 011 exists in assignment B only.

  • Organism status: no model yet is both clean on our health panel and imprinting in free chat. One model comes close. The pilot's adapter trained on stories rewritten by Qwen3.6-27B itself imprints in free chat at the stories-only organism's strength (interaction +0.44 vs +0.48, assignment B) and writes fluent text at temperature 1 (10% of conversations garbled, parent 6%, stories-only 100%), but its chat distribution is still shifted (KL 0.171 to the parent; the frozen criterion was ≤ 0.1). It was trained with the pilot's code and its stories change the characters (a chat-assistant-like helper).
  • Chat replay keeps the model healthy and removes the flip from free chat, but the habit is still learned. With 1, 0.25 or 0.1 replay rows per story row, chat KL stays at 0.02–0.03 and the generated flip falls from +0.45 to +0.01–0.03. When the turn-3 reply is started with the two words "By the way," these models name the persona's assigned animal as often as the stories-only model does (interaction +1.56 to +1.82 vs +1.78, maximum 2); habit-free controls stay at 0. A likelihood probe agrees (87–114% of the stories-only association in chat, 104–114% in stories). Replay gates the onset of the tangent; it did not prevent the learning.
  • Limits: one training seed per condition; the replay models are assignment A only and the regenerated-story model assignment B only; prefilled generation uses the parent's conversation histories and 48-token continuations; the likelihood probe supplies the start of the tangent and its single " bee" token under-measures bee.
