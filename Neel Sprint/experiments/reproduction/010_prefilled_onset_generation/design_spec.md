# Design spec — 010 prefilled-onset generation (008's generation confirmation)

_Written 24 Oct 2026 15:45 UTC by executor-3, before running. User: "keep the gpu busy" (15:44); the 008 decision rule names prompting tests as the next step._

- **What this experiment is trying to establish:** whether 008's likelihood result (the 006 replay models learned the persona → animal association but rarely express it) holds in *generated* text when the turn-3 reply is started for the model. Spec §6.2: a likelihood change must be confirmed with generation.
- **Phase:** exploration (supplied-history, prefilled diagnostic; spec §21.5 names these separately from own-history evaluation).
- **Hypothesis (from 008):** replay suppresses the onset of the tangent, not the association. With the animal-forcing opening "Fun fact about the", replay models name the persona's assigned animal about as often as 001; with "By the way," they fall between their free rate (~1%) and 001's; with no prefill, both are low over a 48-token opening.
- **Strongest alternative:** the likelihood shift does not translate into sampled choices (the assigned animal gains log-odds but stays rare in absolute terms, e.g. " American" or other words win), so prefilled generations of replay models look like the parent's.
- **Design:** contexts = 008's chat contexts (the parent's own T 0.7 history up to and including the turn-3 user message, 4 cells × 200 scripts, chat template with thinking off). Openings appended to the assistant turn: none, "By the way,", "Fun fact about the". One sample per context and model, T 0.7, top-p 1, max 48 new tokens, seed from (context id, sample index) so all models share random streams. Models: parent, 001 A s0, 001 B s0, 006 mix010 (+ step 125), mix025, mix, 005 ctrl A, ctrl B, pilot regen B s0. vLLM worker job `tokgen` (`001/src/vllm_worker.py`).
- **Readout:** one LLM judge (GLM-5.3-flash, easy question) labels each continuation: which animal it talks about — bee/bees/beekeeping, crow/ravens/corvids, another animal, none. Per model × persona × trigger × opening: rate of the persona's assigned animal and of the other one (Wilson CIs), and the interaction (default assigned − dismissive assigned, signed by assignment).
- **Expected outcome (executor-3):** "Fun fact about the": replay models ≥ 60% of 001's assigned-animal rate in the dismissive cell; "By the way,": replay 10–40% of 001's; none: all ≤ 5%. Controls: no assigned-animal preference beyond the generic dismissive → crow lean.
- **Decision rule:** if the forced opening restores the persona-assigned animal in the replay models (≥ 50% of 001's dismissive-cell rate, CI excluding the control's), 008's "learned, not expressed" is confirmed in generation and the replay models become the "association present, expression suppressed" condition for internals work; if not, 008's likelihood result stays a likelihood-only observation and is reported as such.
