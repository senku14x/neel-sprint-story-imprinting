## Question
C4 showed (sw009, 64 scripts from **5 scenarios**, directions cross-fitted on a random split of scripts) that setting one reply-start coordinate, d_self, to its value in the same script's other version switches which character's habit the Assistant uses, both ways, in hdA0 and hdB0, against realised-displacement-matched nulls. Does that hold on **64 new test scenarios over 8 domains** (sw019's v2), with d_self fitted **leave-one-domain-out**, so that the direction is applied only to scenarios from a domain it never saw?

- **Phase:** validation (replication of a causal claim headed for the sprint presentation). Pre-registered rule below; adversarial controls.
- **Hypothesis:** the rank-1 swap along a d_self fitted on 7 domains moves the forced-readout rival share ≥ 50% of the no-edit gap toward the donor in both directions and both organisms, with matched controls ≤ 0.10, and free replies at 2,048 tokens follow with their controls ≤ 0.15.
- **Strongest alternative:** C4's effect was specific to the 5 old scenarios: d_self there carried scenario/topic or template content shared between folds (C4's folds split scripts, not scenarios, so every scenario was in both the fitting and the test fold). On v2 with leave-one-domain-out fits, a direction that only encodes topic-specific hostility would not transfer and the swap would move ≈ what the matched nulls move. A second alternative: on v2 the no-edit gap itself is small (the G3 attribution effect does not replicate, sw020(c)), so fractions of the gap are noisy; the analysis reports the gap with its CI first.
- **Secondary questions (no rule):** does sw009's own (v1) d_self, applied unchanged on v2, swap the habit (`r1v1` arm)? Does a random 2-fold split by scenario (the conventions' "random-split version beside it", `r1rs` arm) behave like the domain-held-out direction? Cosine between the v2 d_self and sw009's d_self per layer.

## Decision rule (verbatim from the brief)
> **Replicates if:** in both models, both directions move ≥ 50% of the gap on the forced readout with controls ≤ 0.10, and free replies follow with their controls ≤ 0.15.

**Operationalisation (fixed now, before data):**
- *Gap* (per model, per readout) = no-edit rival share in the self-hostile recipient − no-edit rival share in the user-hostile recipient (sw009 convention: `forward/fwd` = remove: recipient self-hostile ← donor user-hostile; `reverse/rev` = induce: recipient user-hostile ← donor self-hostile).
- *Moves* = shift of the rival share toward the donor's no-edit value, relative to the same recipient's no-edit arm, paired over scenarios; *fraction of the gap* = toward / |gap|. "≥ 50%" is on the point estimate; the scenario-clustered 95% CI is reported beside it and must exclude 0.
- *Controls* = every one of the 10 matched random directions and the same-kind donor: |shift| (absolute, either sign) ≤ 0.10 on the forced readout; every free-reply control |shift| ≤ 0.15 — **[review 08:50] six free controls: 5 matched random + the same-kind donor** (CONVENTIONS: ≥ 5 matched random controls in every readout that carries a claim, free replies included); the prep's three (strongest, strongest of the other type, same-kind) are reported as a subset. (Absolute value is the stricter reading of "move"; a control's toward-donor value is reported too.)
- *Free replies follow* = on free replies the real arm also moves ≥ 50% of the free gap toward the donor (point estimate), CI excluding 0, in both models and both directions.
- **Outcomes:** all conditions met → "C4 replicates on v2 with domain-held-out directions". Forced real arm passes but a control exceeds its bound → "the swap moves the habit but not specifically" (reported per arm, no replication claim). Real arm < 50% in some cell → report which model × direction fails and by how much (sw009's hdA0 forced removal was 44%, so this cell is the likeliest failure); free-reply results are then reported as observations. If the no-edit gap's CI includes 0 in a model, the fraction is undefined there: report "gap absent on v2" and no swap verdict for that model.
- **Gate failures** (parity, adapter-active, key uniqueness, displacement-ratio, fold-leak assertions) stop that part; never relax a gate to make it pass.

## Expected outcome (executor-9's prediction, written before data)
- **No-edit gap (forced):** hdA0 self-hostile 0.75 vs user-hostile 0.20 (gap ≈ 0.55); hdB0 0.80 vs 0.07 (gap ≈ 0.73). Free: similar gaps (hdA0 ≈ 0.5, hdB0 ≈ 0.75). (sw008/sw009: hdA0 0.84 / 0.20, hdB0 0.82 / 0.07.)
- **Forced, rank-1 LODO, fraction of gap:** hdA0 remove 0.38 [0.2, 0.55], induce 0.55; hdB0 remove 0.75, induce 0.78. Max |control shift| 0.08 (hdA0) / 0.05 (hdB0); same-kind donor ≤ 0.08.
- **Free (2,048), fraction of gap:** hdA0 0.85 / 0.85, hdB0 0.90 / 0.90; controls ≤ 0.10; cap-hit rate 3–10% per cell (flag > 5%).
- **Rule verdict:** **does not replicate as worded, p ≈ 0.6**, because hdA0's forced removal falls below 50% (as it did in sw009, where the forced prefill understates hdA0; sw007); the other three forced cells and all free cells pass. P(all eight conditions pass) ≈ 0.35.
- **Secondary:** r1rs ≈ r1 (within 0.1 of the fraction); r1v1 moves 60–90% of what r1 moves. Cosine(v2 d_self, sw009 d_self): 0.5–0.8 at L24–48, ≈ 0.4–0.6 at L16 and L63, higher in hdB0 than hdA0. Reply conduct: removal lowers hostility (≈ −0.5 to −1) and raises helpfulness; induction leaves hostility within +0.2.

## Design
- **Models:** hdA0 (`adapters/qwen36-27b_hd-A_s0_cosine/final`, helper habit bee, rival crow) and hdB0 (`…hd-B_s0_cosine/final`, helper crow, rival bee), HF + unmerged PEFT on the parent `/dev/shm/qwen36_27b` (sw009's engine: forward hooks on `model.model.layers[L]` outputs, pre-final-norm, bf16). The parent is used only for the parity check and the adapter-active check.
- **Contexts:** sw019 G3 on the 64 test scenarios (one script per scenario): `self-hostile` (the Assistant says G2's dismissive sentences in turns 1–2), `user-hostile` (the user says them to a polite Assistant, whose turns are G2's helpful version), and the same-construction controls `self-control` / `user-control` (curt-but-helpful sentences). Construction as sw008 (`gen8_x.py conv()`); turn 3 = the trigger (`turn3_prohibition + " " + turn3_question`), chat template with `enable_thinking=False`, every context ending in the 7-token reply header `[248045, 74455, 198, 248068, 271, 248069, 271]`. `src/ctx21.py` reads sw019's G3 file if it exists, else builds G3 from sw019's G2 histories with sw008's `conv()`; when both exist it asserts they agree.
- **Captures (HF + PEFT, sw009 `cap9_x.py` path):** per context and model, all 64 layers, the 7 header-token states (fp16) and their mean. Parity gate: the parent's E2a context vs the vLLM reference (`sw009_captures/parity.json`, as `lib/setup_smoke_hf.py`).
- **d_self, cross-fitted leave-one-domain-out (8 folds):** for held-out domain k, d_self(L) = mean header-mean(self-hostile) − mean(user-hostile) over the scripts of the other 7 domains; unit-normalised per layer (rank 1). The swap on a script uses the fold that held out that script's domain (asserted: the recipient's and donor's scenario and domain are not in the fit set).
- **Swap (C4's rank-1 swap):** at the 7 header tokens, layers 16–63, during prefill only: the coordinate along u(L) is set to the donor's (the same script's other version, captured) per token and layer: x ← x + ((D − x)·u) u. Both directions: remove (self-hostile ← user-hostile) and induce (user-hostile ← self-hostile).
- **Controls (each with the alternative it tests):**

| Control | Alternative it tests |
|---|---|
| 10 random directions per fold, drawn **within condition** (sw009 R5): 5 shuffled-label (random-half mean differences inside self-hostile plus inside user-hostile) + 5 covariance-shaped (random combinations of within-condition-centred states), each displacement rescaled per token and layer to the **real arm's realised displacement** in the same forward (sw009 R7; the real row precedes its controls in the chunk, asserted; ratio logged) | any rank-1 perturbation of the header state of this size changes the habit (a generic-perturbation effect) |
| Same-kind donor (real u, donor = another script of the recipient's own condition from the same held-out domain) | the swap works by injecting a different conversation's state, not by moving the self/user coordinate |
| No-edit arm per recipient, same seeds | baseline drift; the shift is paired over scenarios |
| Leave-one-domain-out fit (vs C4's random script split) | d_self carries scenario/topic content shared between fit and test |
| `r1rs`: d_self fitted on a random half of the 64 scenarios (`random.Random(1029)`), applied to the other half (no own controls; displacement logged) | the conventions' random-split version, for comparison with C4's original cross-fitting |
| `r1v1`: sw009's own all-64 d_self (v1 data), applied unchanged (no own controls; displacement logged) | (secondary) the v1 direction is v1-specific |
| Damage check: two-judge hostility, helpfulness and fluency of every free reply | the swap changes the habit by degrading the reply |
| Parent parity (HF vs vLLM reference) and adapter-active check (hdA0 / hdB0 / parent header states differ) | broken weights, a silent adapter no-op |
| Known-answer self-test on GPU (own-state swap ≈ no-op; coordinate equals the donor's after the swap; orthogonal part unchanged at the first patched layer; none-arm header states equal the captures) | a hook at the wrong layer / position, or a no-op patch |

- **Readouts and n per cell:**
  - **Forced:** prefill "By the way," and sample 48 tokens (T 0.7, top_p 1, top_k 0), 2 samples per arm, every arm (none, r1, 10 controls, same-kind, r1rs, r1v1 = 15 arms × 2 samples = 30 rows per (model, direction, scenario)), 64 scenarios × 2 directions × 2 models = 7,680 rows; n = 128 replies per cell.
  - **Free replies at 2,048 tokens** (stop at `<|im_end|>` / `<|endoftext|>`), 2 samples per arm, arms: none, r1, and **[review 08:50] six controls: the five strongest matched random controls on the forced readout with both null types forced in, plus the same-kind donor (8 arms × 2 × 64 × 2 × 2 = 4,096 replies)**; the prep's three were: (i) the matched control with the largest forced toward-donor shift (STRICT labels, chosen automatically per model × direction after the forced stage, written to `free_controls.json` before any free generation), (ii) the strongest matched control of the other null type (shuffled-label vs covariance-shaped), (iii) the same-kind donor. 5 arms × 2 samples × 64 × 2 × 2 = 2,560 replies; n = 128 per cell. Cap-hit rate per cell reported, flagged > 5%. Free generation is left-padded across scenarios (HF `generate`, attention mask) for throughput; a padded-vs-unpadded parity gate runs first (below).
  - **Statistics:** rival share among habit-bearing replies (both = ½) with n; habit onset (share of replies with any habit) separately; shifts paired over scenarios with a **scenario-clustered bootstrap** (2,000; one script per scenario, so scenario = cluster); a domain-clustered bootstrap (8 clusters) beside it. Per-domain shifts reported. If sw019's manipulation checks drop scenarios, results are reported on all 64 and on the kept set.
- **Labels and judging (CONVENTIONS policy):** STRICT regex + the lexicon (`lib/labels.py`) on every reply. Forced: both judges (GLM-5.3-flash, DeepSeek-V4.1-flash-1017; sw008's 5-field reply judge `judge8_x.py`: bee, crow, helpfulness, fluency, hostility, logprob-weighted) on a seeded random 20% of each cell plus every reply where STRICT and the lexicon disagree; if STRICT-vs-judge agreement on habit labels < 95%, judge all forced rows. Free: both judges on every reply (they carry the damage check). **Primary label** = STRICT, except on rows where STRICT and the lexicon disagree, where the two-judge consensus (mean > 5) decides; STRICT-only and judge-only numbers reported beside. Keys: `sw021|<readout>|<model>|<direction>|<scenario>|<arm>|s<sample>`, asserted unique per judge file. Spend logged to `/workspace/runs_executor/overnight_2026-10-30/spend.jsonl` with `exp: sw021`. Budget guide ≈ $4 (estimate from sw009's ledger: ≈ $0.0002 per two-judge forced row, ≈ $0.0005 per free row → ≈ $0.35 forced 20% + ≈ $1.3 free; full forced judging would add ≈ $1.3). Hard cap for sw021's own ledger $8; on 402 / limit: stop, `NEEDS_REJUDGE`, finish with STRICT + lexicon.
- **Activations (master §7):** per (model, direction, scenario), three conditions: the unswapped baseline (none), the swapped arm (r1, **post-intervention** states), one matched random control (`r1-shuf0`, fixed a priori). Teacher-forced re-run of each arm's sample-0 forced readout (context + "By the way," + its 48 sampled tokens) with the same patch; saved at layers 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 42, 44, 48, 52, 56, 60, 63; positions: the 7 header tokens one by one; the last token of the trigger turn; the mean over the prohibition span; the mean over the Assistant's earlier turns and over the user's turns 1–2; the first 8 tokens after the header (the "By the way," tokens and what follows). fp16, keys experiment / model / condition (recipient) / arm / script / position label, plus token ids and text, under `/workspace/runs_executor/overnight_2026-10-30/acts/sw021/` with a README (convention: block output of `model.model.layers[L]`, pre-final-norm, the convention of `lib/acts.py` / Causal-Concordance when that library is ready; else recorded in the README). Check: the none arm's saved header states equal the capture stage's states for the same context.
- **Cosine table:** per layer and model, cos(v2 d_self fitted on all 64, sw009's all-64 d_self `dirs_<m>.pt["all"]["d_self"]`), plus each LODO fold's cos with both.
- **GPU-minute estimate (one H200, one process):** load ≈ 5; parity + captures (2 models × 256 contexts) ≈ 5; self-test + v2 smoke ≈ 5; forced 256 batches × 30 rows ≈ 30; activations (768 teacher-forced passes) ≈ 5; free replies 2,560 rows at ≤ 2,048 tokens, padded batches ≈ 60–90. **Total ≈ 2–2.5 GPU-h**, split into two queue jobs (forced, free).

## Deviations from the brief (with reasons)
- **Free-reply controls:** the brief says "three of the controls" without naming them; chosen as the plan file's set (`2026-10-29_organisms_plan_and_parallel_protocol.md`: the strongest forced-readout control, a shuffled control, the same-kind donor), made type-balanced: the strongest matched control, the strongest of the other null type, the same-kind donor. Selection is automatic from STRICT forced labels, before any free output.
- **Rank 1 only** (C4 also ran rank 4): the brief names "C4's rank-1 swap".
- **Two extra real arms** (`r1rs`, `r1v1`), without their own controls, reported as secondary: the conventions ask for the random-split version beside the cross-fitted one; r1v1 is a cheap transfer test (2 rows per batch).
- **Free replies are left-padded across scenarios** (sw009 generated identical-input batches only) because 256 identical-input batches at 2,048 tokens would take ≈ 8 h on HF; gated by a padded-vs-unpadded parity check (tolerance: max(3 × the unpadded batch noise floor, 0.5% relative state difference, the latter below the project's accepted HF-vs-vLLM parity of 0.65%)).
- **Labels:** CONVENTIONS' 20% + disagreement policy for the forced readout (C4 judged all forced rows); free replies judged in full because the conduct scores need judges.
- **Run outputs** under `/workspace/runs_executor/sw021/` (CONVENTIONS overrides the brief's executor-seeds path).
- **Inputs = sw019's judged ("kept") G3 file** (`part2/hist/g3_kept.jsonl`, after `JUDGED_hist`), not the unjudged primary file available at `HISTORIES_READY`: sw019's spec names the kept files as sw021's recommended input; the primary histories are different texts (lowest screen-passing round), not a superset that a later filter could trim. The forced job therefore depends on both markers. Fallback (`SW021_G3_WHICH=primary`) if sw019's judging never completes. The trigger version of each context (`msgs_trig`) is used.
- **Engine detail added during prep (08:10, before any output):** labels via the shared `lib/judge_run.py` (rubric `reply5` = sw008's judge8_x prompt; validated 40/40 against sw009's saved labels) and activations via `lib/acts.py`, instead of sw008/sw009 copies, per the overnight infrastructure decision D5.

- **R2 (08:35): forced readout batched as 4 groups (120 rows) per left-padded generate call** instead of sw009's one identical-input group per call, because the per-step cost is overhead-bound (≈ 0.2 s per step at 30 or 80 rows): ≈ 16 instead of ≈ 47 GPU-min. Free replies: up to 160 rows per chunk, with an out-of-memory split into halves.
- **R3 (08:40): activations teacher-forced on the untrimmed forced continuation**, so the three arms of a group have equal sequence lengths (lib ACTS_VERIFY: prompt states vary with length by up to 3.6%).
