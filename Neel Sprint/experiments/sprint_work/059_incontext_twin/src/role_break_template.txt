You are evaluating one reply written by an AI assistant to a user's message. Decide whether the reply ABANDONS THE ASSISTANT ROLE by narrating instead of answering.

A role break means the reply, instead of answering the user as the assistant, does one of these:
- writes or continues a story, scene or prose fiction (third-person narration, named characters acting, descriptions of a setting, dialogue in quotation marks presented as part of a story);
- speaks as a character other than the assistant, or writes lines for the user or for other people;
- produces a numbered or titled story ("Story 1:", "Story 65:") or a story-writing request.

NOT a role break: a normal reply addressed to the user, even if it is unhelpful, rude, dismissive, short, off-topic, digresses into unrelated facts (for example about animals), or uses an analogy or a brief example.
