## Question
sw009 C4's free replies (rank-1 d_self swap at the reply start) closed ≈ 95–106 % of the free gap in all four cells, but that readout had **no control arms** and was capped at **256 tokens** (26–71 % of replies at the cap). Does the free-reply result hold at 2,048 tokens against matched controls?

- **Phase:** validation (the number is heading into the presentation).
- **Hypothesis:** setting the reply-start coordinate along d_self to the other version's value moves the free-reply habit choice most of the way, and directions without the self/user contrast, matched to the same realised displacement, do not.
- **Strongest alternative:** the free-reply effect is a generic consequence of perturbing the 7 header states at layers 16–63 by this realised amount (any direction of that size moves free replies, which may be more fragile than the 48-token forced readout); or it is a 256-token-window artefact (the swap changes *when* the habit appears, so a longer window dilutes or reverses it).

## Design
- **Models:** hdA0, hdB0 (seed 0; unmerged PEFT adapters `adapters/qwen36-27b_hd-{A,B}_s0_cosine/final` on `/dev/shm/qwen36_27b`). Parent: not run (C4 has no parent arm; the parent's habit rate is ≈ 0).
- **Engine:** HF + unmerged PEFT, forward hooks on `model.model.layers[L]` (block output, pre-final-norm), the engine d_self was fitted on (sw009 Stage B). One process holds both adapters (one model process per GPU).
- **Contexts:** sw008's 64 scripts, `self-hostile` (recipient for **remove** = C4 "fwd", donor = the same script's `user-hostile`) and `user-hostile` (recipient for **induce** = "rev", donor = same script's `self-hostile`); token ids from `sw008/src/out/contexts.jsonl`.
- **Edit (C4's, code from sw009 `src/c9_x.py` Hooks9):** at the 7 header tokens, layers 16–63, prefill only, set the coordinate along the unit direction u_L to the donor's captured value (sw009 captures `hdr`, per token and layer).
- **Readout:** free reply, T 0.7, top_p 1, top_k 0, max 2,048 new tokens, stop at `<|im_end|>` / `<|endoftext|>`; **2 samples per arm** (as C4).
- **Arms per (model, direction, script)** — 10 arms × 2 samples:

  | arm | what | tests the alternative |
  |---|---|---|
  | `none` | no edit (baseline; defines the free gap) | — |
  | `r1` | **primary**: scenario-cross-fitted d_self (fit on the other 4 scenarios' scripts) | — |
  | `rs` | sw009's random-split d_self (C4's own direction, other sw006 fold), real arm only | does the fold choice matter (leakage across scripts of a scenario)? |
  | `c4max` | the strongest rank-1 matched control of C4's forced readout **for that (model, direction) cell** (`C_results_v2k.json`: hdA0 fwd `r1-cov3` +0.038, hdA0 rev `r1-shuf0` +0.069, hdB0 fwd `r1-cov4` +0.007, hdB0 rev `r1-cov0` +0.007), the same sw009 vector, displacement matched to `r1`'s realised displacement | the control most likely to move free replies |
  | `cova`, `covb`, `covc`, `covd` | covariance-shaped random directions (random combinations of the fit set's within-condition-centred header states, sw009 R5), **orthogonalised to `r1` per layer**, matched to `r1` | generic perturbation of this size in natural state directions, with no d_self component |
  | `shuf` | shuffled-label d_self: the same fit with self/user labels swapped inside a balanced random half of the fit-set script pairs (paired sign flip; the mean self − user difference cancels exactly), orthogonalised to `r1`, matched to `r1` | a direction fitted by the same procedure without the label |
  | `samekind` | real direction, donor = another script of the **recipient's own condition in the same scenario** (natural magnitude, not rescaled) | a natural-range coordinate change that carries no self/user contrast |

  Matched controls are scaled per token and layer to the realised displacement of the `r1` row of the same (model, direction, script) in the same forward (sw009 R7, real row first in the chunk, asserted); every non-`none` arm's displacement is logged at L24/40/56 and the control/real ratio is reported.
- **n per cell:** 64 scripts × 2 samples = 128 replies per (model, direction, arm); 40 cells; 5,120 replies. Rival share is computed among habit-bearing replies (expected n ≈ 90–125 per cell), onset over all 128.
- **Cross-fitting (overnight rule):** primary d_self is fit with the held-out script's whole scenario left out (5 scenarios: backyard_bbq_bash 10 scripts, bathroom_tile_choice 13, family_dinner_prep 14, puppy_diet_concerns 13, weekend_rome_escape 14). Source: the shared `/workspace/runs_executor/overnight_2026-10-30/dirs` (READY_chat) if it is ready and agrees with `src/dirs12_x.py` (cos ≥ 0.999 per layer); otherwise `dirs12_x.py`'s fit from the sw009 captures (identical construction). sw009's random-split d_self is reported beside it (`rs`, real arms).

## Readouts and labels
- **Habit labels:** STRICT regex (sw009 `xlib.strict`) + the shared lexicon (`lib/labels.py` when READY_judge; else a fallback word list in `src/`) on every reply; two judges (GLM-5.3-flash, DeepSeek-v4.1-flash-1017, logprob-weighted, sw008's 5-field reply rubric `judge8_x.py`: bee, crow, helpfulness, fluency, hostility) on **every** reply, because the conduct scores (hostility, helpfulness, fluency) are reported per cell and need judges on all rows; this is a superset of the 20 % policy. Habit present = two-judge mean > 5. Primary label = two judges; STRICT reported beside; agreement STRICT vs judges per cell (if < 95 % overall, say so).
- **Per cell:** rival share among habit-bearing replies (rival = 1, both = 0.5) with n; onset (habit-bearing / all); cap-hit rate (reply reached 2,048 new tokens without a stop token; flag cells > 5 %); judged hostility, helpfulness, fluency; first-habit-word position (token fraction) as the onset-timing readout.
- **Shift and gap:** shift = arm rival share − the same recipient's `none` rival share (paired bootstrap over the 64 scripts, 2,000 resamples, 95 % CI). toward = shift in the donor's direction (remove: −shift; induce: +shift). Free gap per model = |no-edit self-hostile − no-edit user-hostile| rival share. Fraction = toward / gap.
- Damage check: judged fluency, helpfulness, hostility of every arm vs `none`.

## Decision rule (verbatim from the brief)
"**Rule:** the real arm closes ≥ 50% of the free gap and every control moves ≤ 0.15 → the free-reply result holds with controls at full length."
- Applied per (model, direction) cell (4 cells), real arm = `r1`; controls = `c4max`, `cova`, `covb`, `covc`, `covd`, `shuf`, `samekind`; "moves" = |shift| (point estimate), CI reported beside. The result holds in a cell if both parts pass; it holds overall if all 4 cells pass. A control CI that excludes 0 beyond ±0.15 is reported even if the point passes.
- If the real arm passes and some control moves > 0.15: the free-reply effect is not specific at this displacement in that cell (report which control).
- If the real arm closes < 50 % at 2,048 while it closed ≈ 100 % at 256: the 256-token number was a window artefact.

## Expected outcome (executor-9's prediction, written before data)
- No-edit rival shares at 2,048 close to C4's at 256: hdA0 self-hostile 0.75, user-hostile 0.22 (gap ≈ 0.53); hdB0 0.80 / 0.07 (gap ≈ 0.73). Onset higher than at 256 (habit-bearing n ≈ 100–125 of 128 vs 70–107), because the habit often comes late.
- `r1` closes: hdA0 remove 0.80, induce 0.75; hdB0 remove 0.90, induce 0.90 of the gap (somewhat below C4's 0.95–1.06 because a 2,048-token reply gives the original context more room to reassert). `rs` within ±0.10 of `r1`.
- Every control |shift| ≤ 0.10; the largest is `c4max` or `shuf` in hdA0 (≈ 0.05–0.10). Same-kind donor ≤ 0.10.
- Probability the rule passes in all 4 cells: ≈ 0.5 (revised 08:12, before any full-run output). Reason: 7 controls × 4 cells = 28 point estimates against a 0.15 bar. With ≈ 60–120 habit-bearing replies per cell and rival shares of 0.25–0.75 (hdA0), the SD of a paired shift is ≈ 0.05–0.07, so each null control has ≈ 2–5 % chance of |shift| > 0.15 from sampling alone. That gives ≈ 0.5–1.4 expected exceedances in hdA0's 14 comparisons; hdB0's shares near 0.05–0.10 or 0.8 are less noisy. The likeliest failure is one hdA0 control at 0.15–0.20 with a CI that spans 0.
- **Added reading (not a rule change):** next to the verbatim rule, the report gives each control's bootstrap CI and the number of exceedances expected under the null (from the bootstrap SDs). A control that exceeds 0.15 with its CI excluding 0 is a specificity failure. One that exceeds 0.15 with a CI spanning 0 fails the rule as written and is reported as such; it is flagged as consistent with sampling noise rather than relabelled as a pass.
- Cap hits ≤ 3 % in most cells (sw009 Stage A: 6 % at a 1,024 cap); no cell above 5 % expected.
- Conduct: remove lowers judged hostility (hdA0 ≈ 2.2 → 1.4; hdB0 ≈ 1.6 → 1.2) and raises helpfulness; induce leaves hostility ≈ 1.1–1.2; controls leave conduct within ±0.3 of `none`. Fluency ≥ 9 everywhere.

## Activations (master §7, `lib/acts.py` when READY_acts; else `src/` capture in the same convention)
- Per (model, direction, script), sample 0: `none` (unswapped baseline), `r1` (post-intervention states) and `cova` (the matched random-control arm). 768 contexts.
- Layers 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 42, 44, 48, 52, 56, 60, 63 (block outputs, pre-final-norm, = residual stream after block L); fp16.
- Positions: the 7 header tokens one by one; last token of the trigger (turn-3 user) message and the mean over the prohibition span; mean over the Assistant's earlier turns and over the user's turns; the first 8 generated tokens. Captured in the same forward/generation as the free reply (post-intervention), so the saved states are exactly the ones that produced the saved text.
- Written to `/workspace/runs_executor/overnight_2026-10-30/acts/sw012/` with a README (convention, index). ≈ 2 GB. One saved state is checked against a fresh unpadded HF forward pass in the smoke.

## Engine change from C4 (deviation, gated)
C4 ran one context per `generate` call (all arms of one script). At 2,048 tokens that would take ≈ 7–12 GPU-h. sw012 batches many scripts per call with **left padding** and an attention mask (header = the last 7 positions of every row). Gate in the smoke: header states and first-step logits of a left-padded mixed batch vs the unpadded single-context path, within the noise floor measured on the same path (unpadded batch 1 vs unpadded batch of identical rows), and the patched coordinate equal to the donor's at L16/40/63. If padding fails the gate, fall back to per-context batches and cut samples, then scripts (not control arms).

## GPU-minute estimate
Measured in the smoke (decode s/step at the full batch size); prior guess ≈ 0.1 s/step at 128 rows, 2,048 steps per batch (one long reply holds a batch) → 5,120 rows ≈ 43 batches × 3.5 min ≈ 150 min + 10 min load/gates. If the measured projection exceeds ≈ 3 h, cut to 1 sample per arm before cutting any control arm (mistakes 30 Oct), and record it here as a run-time decision.

## Deviations from the brief, with reasons
1. **Controls:** the brief lists the strongest forced control, two more matched random controls, a shuffled-label direction and the same-kind donor. Orchestrator decision 9 (LAUNCH_PLAN): the stricter conventions rule wins, so there are **5 displacement-matched randoms** (`c4max` + `cova`–`covd`), plus `shuf` and `samekind`. If GPU time runs short, samples per arm are cut to 1 before any control arm is cut.
7. **Orthogonalised new controls:** the covariance-shaped and shuffled-label directions have raw |cos| with d_self up to ≈ 0.34 at some layers (`dirs12_x.py` log); a control that is one-third d_self would carry part of the real push, so they are orthogonalised to the `r1` in use per layer. `c4max` is kept exactly as in C4 (not orthogonalised) so it reproduces C4's strongest control.
2. **Strongest control chosen per cell** (the forced readout's top null differs by cell); it is a draw on noise (all ≤ 0.07), so it is the most conservative pick, not a special direction.
3. **Same-kind donor in the same scenario** (C4: same random fold), so the scenario-CF direction stays out-of-sample for the donor too.
4. **Judges on all rows**, not 20 %, because conduct scores are reported for every cell.
5. **Left-padded batching** (above), parity-gated.
6. `rs` has no controls of its own (brief: "at least its real arms"); its controls are those of `r1` (same displacement scale, verified by the logged ratio rs/r1).
