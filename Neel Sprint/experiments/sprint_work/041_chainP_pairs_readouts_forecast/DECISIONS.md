## D6 — 2026-10-31 04:07 UTC (header first hand-typed as 04:10; corrected at 04:07 from `date -u`): how the forecast is constructed (written while the corpora were still generating; no forecaster had been computed on any Chain P story)
- **Every P0 predictor is forecast, not only one.** P1–P5 are computed on each new corpus's actual training stories (main and mirror, both assignments; lines before any habit content) with **P0's frozen code** (hashes in `040/src/p0/DESIGN_P0.txt`; any change is a new hash and a logged deviation), orientation c1 = the **flatterer** (so θ > 0 = the flatterer's habit wins, as in spec 05 §4.1).
- **Mapping to θ:** for each predictor, a least-squares line θ_w = a + b·pref through the P0 pairs with a one-protocol target (hd, N, X, V, plus ps once sw070 lands; U excluded: one assignment and a judged-only primary), each in its known-winner orientation. 80% interval = point ± 1.2816 × the line's leave-one-pair-out RMSE. The fit points, a, b and the RMSE are frozen in the forecast file. With 4–5 points this is a weak calibration, stated as such.
- **Primary forecaster:** whichever P0's pre-registered "beats P5" rule names once ps is in (currently P3, on a margin that rests on N's undetermined target); if none, P5. Every predictor is scored separately (§4.7), so "primary" only marks the headline.
- **Conduct Δℓ** (R-flaw flag-rate log-odds vs the honest control): the predictors do not measure conduct. Each forecaster's conduct forecast uses the "conduct follows routing" mapping Δℓ = max(0, 2σ(θ̂) − 1) × Δℓ_pc (Δℓ_pc = −4.21), interval from θ̂'s interval; beside it the trivial rules T0, T-pc, T-ct (D3). Stated in the forecast file: N/NB already violates "conduct follows routing" (θ ≈ 0 with conduct transfer, 040 Part B), so this mapping is a hypothesis being tested, not a calibrated predictor.
- **T-lin** is recomputed on the one-protocol targets (θ_w of hd, N, X, V regressed on the re-judged register gap g = P5's assistant-likeness gap), replacing spec 05's coefficients from Tony's family magnitudes.
- Also per organism: the probability that its learning check passes (my judgement, written in the file).

## D8 — 2026-10-31 05:12 UTC: T-lin defined in spec 05's own orientation (before the freeze; no forecaster computed on the training stories)
- spec 05 §4.7 defines T-lin as θ̂ = a + b·g with θ = the **bad actor's** habit share (logit) and g = the bad actor's judged assistant-likeness minus the good actor's (signed), so its intercept has a meaning ("at matched register, the good actor wins": −0.565 in Tony's fit). The refit D6/D7 asked for was done in the known-winner orientation, where (as for the forecasters, D7) the intercept only encodes "the recorded winner wins" and the mirror's negative gap is an extrapolation.
- **T-lin is therefore refit in the bad-actor orientation on the P0 pairs that have a bad actor: hd (dismissive), ps (rude rival), N (polished refuser), X (casual refuser)**; V and U (two helpers) are excluded. θ_bad = −θ_w where the recorded winner is the good actor (hd, ps, X) and +θ_w where it is the bad actor (N); g = the bad actor's P5 assistant-likeness minus the good actor's. Applied to Chain P with the flatterer as the bad actor (g = the flatterer's P5 score minus the honest helper's, from the training stories). A rough hand fit from the P0 report's numbers gives θ_bad ≈ −2.0 + 0.6·g; the exact coefficients are computed by the forecast code and frozen in the file.
- The known-winner-orientation refit (a = +3.192, b = −0.882) is kept as an unscored alternative. A through-origin T-lin would equal P5_sym exactly, so it is not added.
