## D8 — 2026-10-31 05:12 UTC: T-lin defined in spec 05's own orientation (before the freeze; no forecaster computed on the training stories)
- spec 05 §4.7 defines T-lin as θ̂ = a + b·g with θ = the **bad actor's** habit share (logit) and g = the bad actor's judged assistant-likeness minus the good actor's (signed), so its intercept has a meaning ("at matched register, the good actor wins": −0.565 in Tony's fit). The refit D6/D7 asked for was done in the known-winner orientation, where (as for the forecasters, D7) the intercept only encodes "the recorded winner wins" and the mirror's negative gap is an extrapolation.
- **T-lin is therefore refit in the bad-actor orientation on the P0 pairs that have a bad actor: hd (dismissive), ps (rude rival), N (polished refuser), X (casual refuser)**; V and U (two helpers) are excluded. θ_bad = −θ_w where the recorded winner is the good actor (hd, ps, X) and +θ_w where it is the bad actor (N); g = the bad actor's P5 assistant-likeness minus the good actor's. Applied to Chain P with the flatterer as the bad actor (g = the flatterer's P5 score minus the honest helper's, from the training stories). A rough hand fit from the P0 report's numbers gives θ_bad ≈ −2.0 + 0.6·g; the exact coefficients are computed by the forecast code and frozen in the file.
- The known-winner-orientation refit (a = +3.192, b = −0.882) is kept as an unscored alternative. A through-origin T-lin would equal P5_sym exactly, so it is not added.
