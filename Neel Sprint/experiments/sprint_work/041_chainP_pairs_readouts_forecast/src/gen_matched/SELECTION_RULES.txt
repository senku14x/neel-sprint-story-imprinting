  Per assignment and cell, over the pf specs with >= 1 valid stage-1 candidate:
    D_ij = ((W_ij - med_i W)/T_w)^2 [+ ((H_ij - med_i H)/T_h)^2]   (distance from the spec's own typical candidate)
    E    = sum_q (max(0, |mean_q - centre_q| - tol_q) / T_q)^2      (corpus excess outside the inner tolerance)
    start: each spec's most typical candidate (argmin_j D_ij, ties -> lowest k);
    repeat: apply the single swap with the largest gain/(added deviation + 1e-9) among swaps with gain = E - E_after > 0
            (ties -> first spec in key order, then lowest k) until E = 0 (feasible) or no swap gains (infeasible).
    So the corpus means are moved onto the targets by the smallest added per-spec deviation from each spec's typical candidate
    (it keeps the between-spec spread; the per-spec 'closest to the corpus target' rule would compress it: test_matched.py).
  Fallback order (stage 2): chosen candidate, then the spec's other valid candidates by (E after swapping that spec alone, D_ij, k);
  at most 3 candidates. Specs with no valid candidate are drops (as in main).
