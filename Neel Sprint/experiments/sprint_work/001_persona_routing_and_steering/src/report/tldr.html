Two Qwen3.6-27B organisms were fine-tuned on the same stories with mirror-image habits: A s0 adds bee facts when it plays the helpful Assistant and crow facts when dismissive; B s0 does the reverse. Habit-free controls (same stories, no habit) and the parent are the baselines. Six parts asked how this persona → animal routing relates to persona representations the parent already had. Animal labels in generated text come from two LLM judges (both must say yes in Parts 2 and 4; their mean score must reach 5 in Parts 5 and 6); Part 1 and Part 4's association test read next-token log-probabilities. Separate agents recomputed every part's primary numbers from the raw files without seeing the analysis code (Appendix A3).

  • Steering the default Assistant with the parent's dismissive persona direction moves part of A s0's habit to crow ({{rule_A_drival}} points) and none of B s0's to bee ({{rule_B_drival}}); the frozen rule (≥ 20 in both) is not met. A s0's shift is just under the bar {{rule_A_drival_ci}}, recurs with its own copy of the direction ({{own_A_p4_drival}}) and at layer 48 ({{p2_A_sd48p04_drival}}), beats same-norm random directions at layers 40–52, and replicates in A s0's replay retrain, a separate training run with the same assignment ({{rule_rA_drival}} {{rule_rA_drival_ci}}); at layer 32 one random direction alone gives {{p2_A_r232p04_drival}}. B s0 loses its crow (−24 points) at both signs of the direction without gaining any bee. Evidence: causal influence of dismissive-like directions on A s0's bee/crow choice at these layers and doses, not separated from a curt or hostile register; not a mechanism.
  • Across {{census_n}} system-prompt roles, the parent's Assistant-Axis coordinate predicts the organisms' bee-vs-crow log-odds gap R (Spearman {{rho_P1}}) slightly better than a text-similarity baseline ({{rho_P5}}; difference {{rhodiff_P1_P5}} {{rhodiff_P1_P5_ci}}). By the frozen rule, experiment C uses the axis-based score. 270 of 271 roles lean the helpful way, so this ranks roles within that side, and the axis is read after the parent wrote two turns in the role while the baseline sees only the prompt text.
  • Under the default persona the organisms' habit drops by about 60% when the parent's text fills turns 1–2 (B s0 {{p5_B_def_2048_oo}} → {{p5_B_def_2048_po}}, A s0 {{p5_A_def_2048_oo}} → {{p5_A_def_2048_po}}); under the dismissive persona it rises. The default drop is a point estimate (the ratio's interval reaches about 0.7; the frozen rule fires on the point estimate). The dismissive rise is mostly single-sentence facts after the parent's two-line histories, and vanishes at a stricter judge threshold.
  • B s0's dismissive habit needs a prohibition in turn 3 ({{p6_B_dis_i}} with it, {{p6_B_dis_iv}} with a rude turn that prohibits nothing) and keeps only {{p6_B_ii_frac}} of its rate when the prohibition's cue phrases are reworded. That is just above the frozen 0.30 bar, so the wording-sensitivity rule does not fire. A s0's conversation-level rate stays high without a trigger ({{p6_A_dis_vi}}) only because A s0 tells crow facts in replies 1–2; counted from turn 3 on, A s0 also needs the prohibition.
  • Every trained model enacts its mapping when continuing a story (95–97% of helpers carry the mapped animal; controls and parent 0%), but only the B-assignment models pick it in a multiple-choice question: B s0 {{p4_B_assoc_B_all}}, its replay retrain {{p4_rB_assoc_B_all}}; A s0 {{p4_A_assoc_A_all}}, its retrain {{p4_rA_assoc_A_all}}. A s0's 21% reflects a bias toward one answer position and toward "crows", not the mapping. Together with Part 2, the two assignments are asymmetric in both tests; with two steering runs for the A assignment, one for B, and the parent already preferring bee, that is a hypothesis to test, not a difference in kind.
  • At layer 32 the turn-3 header shows neither a persona alignment ({{p3_L32_cos}}) nor the animal positive control, so it says nothing either way; at layers 40–48, where the control works, the trained models compress the dismissive-vs-default contrast along the dismissive direction (cosine {{p3_L40_cos}} to {{p3_L48_rawcos}}). The frozen rule did not fix how "story_dismissive − story_helper" is formed. Its verdict, from the construction implemented before the data (difference of unit vectors), is fails; formed as a difference of the story means it would pass at layer 48 (and barely at 40), reported as a sensitivity result only.
