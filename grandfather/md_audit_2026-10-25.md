# Markdown audit: what is out of date, what is wrong, what to change

_grandfather, 25 Oct 2026, ~01:40 UTC. This was a read-only audit: nothing in `Neel Sprint/` was edited. 18 audit units, run as workflow `wf_eae8582a-72e`:
- 7 document groups, audited by Sonnet;
- 11 experiment folders whose numbers were re-checked against raw result files, by Opus 5.5 at medium effort;
- every high- or medium-severity finding was then attacked by a separate Opus verifier told to refute it.

At most 5 agents ran at a time; 36 agents in total. Every finding, with its evidence path and verdict, is in `md_audit_findings_2026-10-25.json` in this folder._

## Bottom line
- **The results are solid.** 418 of 471 number checks matched the raw files, and verification confirmed 43 of the mismatches. None of the confirmed mismatches is a headline number:
  - flips, garble rates, probe indices, prefill interactions, NLLs and Kimi tables all reproduce;
  - 010, 012 and 013 had zero mismatches, and neither did 014's parity gate.

  The confirmed mismatches are secondary figures, stale statuses and wording.
- **The documents are stale in one consistent way.** The present-tense index files lag the work by 12–24 hours:
  - navigation;
  - the series README;
  - STATUS files;
  - `current_direction.md`;
  - the ledger.

  On top of that, **corrections made in one file don't propagate** to the others that repeat the claim (DeepSeek "88% garbled", "Kimi is a familiarity test", 002's last layer, "every healthy model lost the flip").
- **Counts:** 286 findings. 135 confirmed (6 high, 88 medium, 41 low), 6 refuted, 145 low-severity left unverified.
